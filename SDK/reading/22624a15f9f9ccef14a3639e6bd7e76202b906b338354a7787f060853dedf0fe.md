    <div id="i-body-content" class="i-body-content" data-source-node="n40">
        <p data-source-node="n41">The Inventory Web Service allows access to links (URL or URI template) to other web services in the inventory functional area.</p>

        <li data-source-node="n51"><strong data-source-node="n52">Method:</strong> GET</li>

        <li data-source-node="n53"><strong data-source-node="n54">Accept:</strong> application/json or application/xml</li>
