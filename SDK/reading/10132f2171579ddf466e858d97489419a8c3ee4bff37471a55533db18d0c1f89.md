        <div class="summary" data-source-node="n41">
    The following web services are all related to the Dock Management functional area of the application:
</div>

<p data-source-node="n42"></p>

<p data-source-node="n43"></p>

<table class="members" data-source-node="n44">
    <tbody data-source-node="n45">
        <tr data-source-node="n46">
            <th class="nameColumn" width="144" data-source-node="n47">Web Service</th>

            <th class="descriptionColumn" data-source-node="n48">Description</th>
        </tr>

        <tr data-source-node="n49">
            <td width="154" data-source-node="n50"><a href="8b6144b0004dcba9f529fc54319222ad8ade1ef2bed48dcd1f116f4824eeb1b3.html" data-source-node="n51">Locate Shipping Containers</a></td>

            <td data-source-node="n52">Takes a list of top-level shipping containers (a.k.a. tree units), determines where each container should be placed based on the appropriate Dock Management Flow, and then invokes the TransferShippingContainers operation.</td>
        </tr>

        <tr data-source-node="n53">
            <td data-source-node="n54"><a href="f42da37fe1ae0372608cdd41d016dc45f921e225b644ad9a27d2ea20d5ef8741.html" data-source-node="n55">Process Dock Assignment WaveStep</a></td>

            <td data-source-node="n56">Called by the Dock Assignment Wave Step, this operation invokes the LocateShippingContainers operation for all top-level containers in the wave.</td>
        </tr>

        <tr data-source-node="n57">
            <td data-source-node="n58"><a href="a10aede293772e497cd1455cd43768a16b5fd0ff17a5a3599b03111a8cebf79f.html" data-source-node="n59">Transfer Shipment Headers</a></td>

            <td data-source-node="n60">Takes a list of shipment headers and a destination location and then invokes the TransferShippingContainers operation for every associated top-level container.</td>
        </tr>

        <tr data-source-node="n61">
            <td data-source-node="n62"><a href="fbc4de4bbb3a0ce764b1f6fa1b47040982307cd714ae996ed93a4993db600510.html" data-source-node="n63">Transfer Shipping Containers</a></td>

            <td data-source-node="n64">Takes a list of top-level shipping containers (a.k.a. tree units) and either assigns new work, reassigns existing work, or pre-assigns future moves to the given destination location(s).</td>
        </tr>
