<h1 data-source-node="n42">Topic Not Found</h1>

<p data-source-node="n43">The link you just clicked was not able to connect to the target page.</p>

<p data-source-node="n44">Some possible reasons and remedies are as follows:</p>

<table class="msdn" cellspacing="0" data-source-node="n45">
    <tbody data-source-node="n46">
        <tr valign="top" data-source-node="n47">
            <th width="47%" data-source-node="n48">Reason</th>

            <th width="53%" data-source-node="n49">Remedy</th>
        </tr>

        <tr valign="top" data-source-node="n50">
            <td width="47%" data-source-node="n51">Destination topic is not part of the area you specified in the <b data-source-node="n52">Filtered by</b> field.</td>

            <td width="53%" data-source-node="n53">Either select another filter or select <b data-source-node="n54">(no filter)</b>.</td>
        </tr>

        <tr valign="top" data-source-node="n55">
            <td width="47%" data-source-node="n56">The URL of the destination topic is incorrect or has changed.</td>

            <td width="53%" data-source-node="n57">Use the index or the search function instead.</td>
        </tr>

        <tr valign="top" data-source-node="n58">
            <td width="47%" data-source-node="n59">Problems with the help installation.</td>

            <td width="53%" data-source-node="n60">Rerun Setup for the product this help is related to and select the 'Repair' option.</td>
        </tr>
    </tbody>
</table>

<p data-source-node="n61">Additional help is available for filters, search, and the index.</p>
