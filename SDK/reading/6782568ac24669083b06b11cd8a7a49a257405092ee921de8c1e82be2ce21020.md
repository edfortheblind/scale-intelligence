    <div id="i-body-content" class="i-body-content" data-source-node="n46">
        <p data-source-node="n47">Screen parts simply represent a portion of an overall web page. Screen parts are represented by the SCREEN_PART database table. There can be 1-N screen parts (ordered by SEQUENCE field) for a particular page. Screen parts are tied to screens through the MAIN_UI_SCREEN table (SCREEN_PART.SCREEN_ID = MAIN_UI_SCREEN.OBJECT_ID).</p>

            </tr>

            <tr data-source-node="n108">
