    <div id="i-body-content" class="i-body-content" data-source-node="n46">
        <p data-source-node="n47">Adding a Multi-Selection Combo Box control to the search pane on an insight page simply requires adding a new/existing sub-accordion SCREEN_GROUP and a new Multi-Selection Combo Box SCREEN_CONTROL record.</p>
