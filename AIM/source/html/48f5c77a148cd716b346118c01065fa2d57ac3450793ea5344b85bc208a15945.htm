Shift Time

Shift Time records are used to define and manage time tracking parameters specific to a warehouse. These settings help accurately record and monitor worker shifts within the designated warehouse environment.

 

Create new Shift Time

  • Warehouse - Choose the warehouse where the worker is assigned. This links the shift time data directly to the appropriate facility.

  • Shift- Select the shift that corresponds to the worker’s scheduled working hours.

  • Description- Provide a clear description of the shift to help identify its purpose or any special notes related to it.

  • Time Zone - Specify the time zone relevant to the warehouse location.

  • Start and End Time - Define the exact start and end times of the shift. This determines the duration of the worker’s scheduled shift within the warehouse.
