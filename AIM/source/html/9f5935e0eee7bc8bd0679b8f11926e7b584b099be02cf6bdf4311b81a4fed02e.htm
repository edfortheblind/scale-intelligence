Before You Begin: Defining Statuses

Before you begin defining statuses in the system, carefully review your current processing for all item categories. Do any items require special handling or refrigeration? Do you manage fragile, expensive or hazardous items? How do you manage these unique items through your warehouse? Before defining statuses in the system, identify all possible processing steps required for your product.

 

 

Prerequisites...

Before you begin defining statuses in the system, please complete the prerequisites.

  1. Map out the processes for each item category. Using a word processor or paper, write down the current types of processing completed in your warehouse. It might be helpful to begin this process by item category: how are refrigerated items handled, how are fragile items handled, etc. This list should include all steps that occur from the time an item enters the pool until its physically leaves the warehouse.
     
  2. Organize all the steps into one master list. Assign a status number to each step in sequential order. The steps should be organized in the order in which they should occur; for example, picking appears before packing because you must pick product before packing it. Based on when they occur, assign a status number to each step in the processes.
