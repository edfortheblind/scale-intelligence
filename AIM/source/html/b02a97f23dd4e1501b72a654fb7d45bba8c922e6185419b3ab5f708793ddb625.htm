Creating/Managing Purchase Orders in Trading Partner Management

Purchase orders indicate what item(s) your warehouse should be expecting to arrive at the receiving dock. PO's are used by a warehouse's suppliers/vendors to supply receipt information ahead of the arrival of the truck to their warehouse. You can create and manage purchase orders via the web in the Trading Partner Management module. Once a purchase order is created, you can generate shipments from lines in the PO itself. This is done via the web's Shipment Entry screen, which can be accessed from the Purchase Order Management screen or the Trading Partner Management Main Menu. Once these "shipments" are passed into SCALE, they become "receipts."

 

For example, suppose your warehouse's supplier is planning on sending you 500 pallets of Item A over the course of a year. They would first create a purchase order for Item A, adding a quantity of 500. When they are ready to ship some of Item A to your warehouse, they would create a shipment associated with this PO, which would indicate how many of the item they are going to send out. When the year is over, and all the Item A quantity has been shipped, the supplier would close the purchase order.

 

From the Purchase Order Management screen, you can also print two documents:  one that will display the PO information, and one that will display a shipment's (receipt) information. Both of these documents will spawn a separate web page window, allowing the user to print from their browser.  

 

NOTE: Remember that "shipments" in TPM become "receipts" when they enter SCALE.

To Start...

Log into Trading Partner Management. Choose the Purchase Order Management option from the Trading Partner Management Main Menu. If it is the first time that you have logged onto the Purchase Order module, you will need to agree with the license agreement. When you press OK, the system will display the primary purchase order management screen.

 

 

 

Primary Purchase Order Screen Actions...

 

Searching For Purchase Orders

You can use the fields on the primary purchase order management screen to search for specific purchase order records. When the system displays your search results, you can perform actions on the purchase orders (these are described in the procedures listed below).

Purchase Order Results Screen Actions...

 

Adding Shipment(s) to a Purchase Order

You can add any number of shipments to a purchase order. You can do this on the purchase order results screen, by choosing the "page" icon on the right side of the results line. Note that if you can only add items to a shipment if they are already associated with a purchase order. For detailed procedures on adding shipments, see the topic "Entering a Web Shipment."

 

Editing Purchase Orders

You can edit the details of a purchase order by choosing thefirst icon on the right side of the results line.

 

Printing a Purchase Order Document

You can print a document that displays a purchase order and its associated shipments.

 

Closing Purchase Orders

You can close a purchase order by choosing the "checkmark" icon on the right side of the results line. When you close a purchase order, the system will ask you to confirm that you want to do this action. Keep in mind that if you close a PO, then you cannot make any further edits or add any additional shipments.

 

Deleting Purchase Orders

You can delete a purchase order by choosing the "X" icon on the right side of the results line. When you delete a PO, the system not only deletes the PO record but any associated shipments as well.

 

 

 

Shipment Information Screen Actions...

 

Printing a Shipment Information Document

You can print a document that displays a purchase order's shipment (and its details).

 

Deleting Shipments

You can delete a purchase order's shipment by choosing the "X" icon on the right side of the results line.
