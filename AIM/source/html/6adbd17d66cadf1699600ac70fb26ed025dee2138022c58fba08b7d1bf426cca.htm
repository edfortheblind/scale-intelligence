Master BOL Type Field

This field indicates what kind of master BOL you want the system to create:

  • Consolidator: This type of master BOL is used when you have a number of shipments going to different final destinations, but are all transported to a consolidation point (a "freight consolidator") first. The master BOL would then display the total shipments destined for this consolidator, and each shipment's BOL will show the shipment's final destination. Note that the consolidator address will display as the "Ship To Address" on the master BOL document.
     
  • Multi Stop TL: This type of master BOL is used for a multi stop truck load (TL). In this case, the master BOL document displays the shipments for the entire trip, and each shipment’s BOL represents one of the stops along the delivery route. NOTE: For the "Ship To Address" on the master BOL, the system will display the address on the shipment that has the highest stop sequence number.
     
  • None: Choose this option if you do not want to generate a master BOL document.
