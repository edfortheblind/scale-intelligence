Splitting a Shipment

For in-pool shipments, the system will allow you to split both whole and partial detail lines to a new shipment. When you split a line from its shipment, the system generates a new shipment and assigns the detail line to it. This new shipment will have the same Shipment ID as the original shipment. However, its Internal Shipment Number will be unique. After the shipment is split, you can manually assign the shipments to different waves. The system will never automatically assign the split shipments to different waves.

Split Shipments using Planned Shipment Insight...

 

  1. Access the Planned Shipment Insight screen and find a shipment which is in In Pool status.

  2. Select the shipment and click on the button Split shipment. This opens the Split shipment transaction screen.

  3. Select the detail line(s) that you want to split from this shipment.
  • To split an entire detail line, click on the checkbox that appears in the first column of the grid and click on the Split button.

  • To split quantities across two shipments, enter any quantity less than or equal to the actual quantity of the shipment line in the split quantity column and then click on the Split button.

  • To split multiple shipment lines to a new shipment, select the multiple rows by clicking on the respective checkboxes and then click on Split button.
