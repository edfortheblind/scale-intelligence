Unit of Measure Field / UM Field

In the system, all product is processed based on its quantity's unit of measure.

 

 

In Work...

The system can track work based on the UM units completed during an hour. On the Estimated Work Rates Window, you can use the Hourly Rate Field to identify how many UM units should be processed for work type/work zone combination. For example, if the Hourly Rate Field contains the value 5 and the Unit of Measure Field contains the value Case the ideal processing output for the work type/work zone combination would be 5 cases per hour.

 

 

For Storage Containers...

This field identifies a storage container used to hold product in a location. All UMs are defined on the Unit of Measure Window. They are associated with items on the Item Unit of Measure Window. Later, they are associated with locations on the Item Location Capacity Window.

 

 

In Shipments...

This field identifies the unit of measure that your company uses to identify the weight, volume or length of items or containers. All UMs are defined on the Unit of Measure Window.
