Bill of Lading (BOL)

A bill of lading is a legal document that is required for all LTL shipments. It includes such information as the ship from and ship to address, carrier information and a cargo listing. Bills of lading can be automatically generated during the paperwork wave step. You can manually generate a bill of lading in the Shipment Insight screen.

 

 

Count/Weight Displays...

The bill of lading displays the count/weight according to VICS standards whenever there is a second level of packing of containers. When there is only top level, the system counts the number of containers and its weight. When there is second level, the system count the number of containers/weight at the second level. The customer statistics shows the containers/weight at the second level . The carrier statistics grand total weight actually adds the empty weight of the parent along with the total weight of the containers. The examples below outline this:
