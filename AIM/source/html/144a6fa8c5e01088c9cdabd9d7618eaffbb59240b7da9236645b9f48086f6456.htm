Reviewing Rating Services

A rating service identifies a carrier/service combination. Progistics requires this information to assign a rate, based on the carrier and service used. For SCALE Less Than Truckload internal rating, this window identifies the rating id and server symbol used. This is shipped with the base SCALE product. Rating services are not required for LTL internal rating.
