
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor875" data-source-node="n57"></a>Manifest Unmanifested Containers During Ship Confirmation Checkbox</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This checkbox is used during ship confirmation. It indicates if the 
 system should attempt to manifest any containers that are currently un-manifested. 
 Typically, this setting would be used in situations where the shipment 
 and its container(s) have not been closed at the time of ship confirmation. </p>
