
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor268" data-source-node="n57"></a>Performing an EDI Transmission</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">An EDI (Electronic Data Interchange) transmission sends manifest data 
 electronically to the carrier. The transmission includes any piece of 
 information that is associated with the manifest. Whether 
 or not you are required to perform a transmission depends on the carrier 
 associated with the manifest. When you close the manifest of a FedEx or UPS shipment, the system 
 sends the EDI transmission automatically. The United States Postal Service 
 (USPS) does not require an EDI transmission at this time. You send an EDI transmission when you close a manifest. To transmit during the closing process, see <a href="bcb0a6ebc704fc613fcf9f8a284006af4bf15ccbd0ebd7f80fccf3c7717044a1.html" data-source-node="n59">Processing a Manifest</a>.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n61"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n62"> </p>
        <h4 data-source-node="n63">Prerequisites...</h4>
        <ul data-source-node="n64">
            <li data-source-node="n65">A manifest must be closed before you can perform 
 an EDI Transmission. If the Transmit Status of the manifest is not available 
 (N/A), then that carrier has not been set up to transmit EDI data.</li>
        </ul>
