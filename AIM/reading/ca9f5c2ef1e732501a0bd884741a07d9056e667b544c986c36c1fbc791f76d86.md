
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor830" data-source-node="n57"></a>World Ease ID Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the group that UPS has assigned to a container 
 during the manifesting process. UPS assigns containers to a certain group 
 based on (1) If the World Ease accessorial is applied; and (2) which hub 
 (intermediate consignee) the shipment is assigned to be shipped to. </p>
