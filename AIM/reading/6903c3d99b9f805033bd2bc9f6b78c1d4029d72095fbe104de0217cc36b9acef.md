
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor2060" data-source-node="n57"></a>Apply Per Container Checkbox</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This checkbox indicates how an accessorial charge will be assigned. 
 </p>
        <ul type="disc" class="ul_1" data-source-node="n59">
            <li data-source-node="n60"><span class="span_3" data-source-node="n61">If the checkbox 
 is selected</span>, the accessorial will be assigned individually to each 
 shipping container. This level is generally selected for parcel carriers.<br data-source-node="n62"> </li>
            <li data-source-node="n63"><span class="span_16" data-source-node="n64">If 
 this checkbox is not selected</span><span class="span_6" data-source-node="n65">, 
 the accessorial will be assigned to the entire shipment. This level is 
 generally selected for LTL and TL carriers.</span>
            </li>
        </ul>
