
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n57"><a name="kanchor418" data-source-node="n58"></a>Work Initiation Method Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n59">This field indicates how work instructions will be generated 
 on an RF device. Work instructions are initiated by either the user or 
 the system.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> </p>

                <td valign="top" class="BodyD-Column1-Body1" data-source-node="n68">

 scan/input the barcode that he/she wants to process. </p>
