
        
        <h1 style="text-align: left;font-size: 12pt" data-source-node="n57">Yard Location</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">You can use this window to create yard locations. This type of location consists of an area and one or several position(s), and are not inventory-tracked. Yard locations are used as a temporary storage area for truck trailers that are going to be received into your warehouse. Once a trailer is residing in a yard location, workers then can move the trailers to other locations in the yard, or move them to the Receiving Dock for processing.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">Create Yard Location</p>
        <p data-source-node="n61"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n62">Use the Warehouse Field to indicate where the new location will reside.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n63">Use the Location Area Field to indicate in which area you want to create your new location.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n64">Use the Location Separator Field to view what character will separate the location area and position values when displaying the concatenated value in yard management processing.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n65">Use the Position Range Field to create a single position or a range of positions. Note that you need to have at least one position for every location you create.</p>

        <p data-source-node="n68"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n69"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n70">Use the Check Digit Field to indicate the check digit that you want a worker to enter into the system to verify he/she visited the correct location.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n71">Use the Generate Check Digit Checkbox to indicate that you want the system to automatically generate check digits for this location.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n72">Use the Multi Trailer Checkbox to indicate that you can have more than one trailer residing in a particular yard location.</p>
