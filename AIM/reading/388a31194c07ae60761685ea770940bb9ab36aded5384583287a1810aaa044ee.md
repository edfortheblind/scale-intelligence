
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n57"><a name="kanchor2106" data-source-node="n58"></a>Strategy Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n59">This field identifies the replenishment strategy used to replenish location(s). 
 A replenishment strategy determines how the system will attempt to replenish 
 product, based on one operational requirement or directive. Each strategy 
 represents a specific system algorithm that the system will use to replenish 
 product. </p>
