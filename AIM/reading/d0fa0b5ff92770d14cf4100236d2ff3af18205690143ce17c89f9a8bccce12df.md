
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor2042" data-source-node="n57"></a>Returns Tracking Number Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field is a unique identifier assigned to a parcel when is manifested. 
 It is used for UPS returns processing. Note that this field will not have 
 a value in it unless the UPS Return accessorial is assigned to a container, 
 and the container is manifested.</p>
