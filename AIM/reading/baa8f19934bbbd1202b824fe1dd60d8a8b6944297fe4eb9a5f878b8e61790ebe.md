
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1350" data-source-node="n57"></a>Parties Field</h1>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">This field identifies a relationship between 
 the U.S. exporter and the foreigh consignee, such as a parent or sister 
 company. A relationship is considered to exist if either party owns at 
 least 10% of the other party. This value applies to international shipments 
 and appears on the Shipper's Export Declaration Form. </p>
