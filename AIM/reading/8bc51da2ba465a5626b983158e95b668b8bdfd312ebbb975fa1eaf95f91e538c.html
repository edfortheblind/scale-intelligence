Substitute Item Field

Use this field to associate substitute item(s) with the current item. You can use substitute item(s) to replace the original item if there is not enough quantity available in the warehouse. When entering an order/shipment line, the system will alert you that the desired item is not in stock, and that a substitute item exists. The user can then decide whether or not to use the substitute item on their order/shipment line.

- You can enter more than one substitute item (the system will add it to the list box under the Substitute Item Field); the system will process through these items in sequential order, and choose the first one that can completely fulfill the original item's requested quantity.

 

- The item(s) you choose as substitutes must have the same company designation as the original item.

 

- Only one level of substitution is supported. For example, suppose Item A has Item B defined as its substitute, and Item B in turn has Item C defined as its substitute. The system will not suggest Item C when there isn't enough inventory for Item A or Item B. The system will suggest Item B if Item A is lacking, but there is enough quantity of Item B to meet the request. The system will make no suggestion when neither Item A nor Item B can meet the request; Item A will be applied to the detail, and allocation of Item A may fail during the wave if inventory is not available at that time.

 

- The system will suggest a substitute only if the substitute item defined can fulfill the entire shipment line quantity. For example, suppose Item A has Item B defined as its substitute, and the available inventory is 5 EA for Item A and 5 EA for Item B. If the shipment line has a request for 10 EA of Item A, the system will not substitute the remaining 5 EA with Item B.
