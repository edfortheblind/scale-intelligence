        <p class="p_12" data-source-node="n97"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n98">Access the Work Order Filters window. Name this record and provide 
 a description.</p>

            <li value="1" data-source-node="n100">Using the Filter

 be displayed under the Work Order Insight Screen's Basic Criteria. </li>
            <li value="2" data-source-node="n101">When finished, 
 press OK. The system will save your custom view.</li>
