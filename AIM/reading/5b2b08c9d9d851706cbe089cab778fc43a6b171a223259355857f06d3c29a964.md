
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1668" data-source-node="n57"></a>Total Cube Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The cube associated with a particular shipment 
 or shipping load. Cube = length x width x height. The field also identifies the number of total 
 cube associated with any value defined on the shipment record, 
 such as carrier, shipment date or customer. For example, you can use this 
 field to determine the total cube associated with a particular customer's 
 shipment.</p>
