
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56">Confirm Button</h1>
        <h5 data-source-node="n57">On the Single Unit Packing Screen section...</h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">This button is enabled only on scanning a valid item. The system process the following on clicking the confirm button.</p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <ul data-source-node="n60">
            <li data-source-node="n61">Closes the container and updates the quantity to pack to zero in tote detail table.</li>
            <li data-source-node="n62">The system may print documents/labels for this container based on the <i data-source-node="n63">Auto Print at Close</i> configured on your packing preference.</li>
            <li data-source-node="n64">When last item in the tote is packed, the tote condition will be moved from <b data-source-node="n65">In Packing</b> to <b data-source-node="n66">Packed</b>.</li>
            <li data-source-node="n67">Transaction history record is written.</li>
        </ul>
