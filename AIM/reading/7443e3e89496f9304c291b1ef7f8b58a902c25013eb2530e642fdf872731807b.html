The following is a discussion of how yard management can be used in your warehouse.

 

 

Guard: Yard Check In

At the time a trailer arrives, the guard (or other warehouse personnel) will check the trailer into the yard. The guard will look up incoming receipts using the Receipt Insight, or enter the trailer ID directly into the Yard Check In/Out Window. Please note that the receipt must have a trailer ID assigned to it in order to be eligible to be checked into the yard. During this check-in process, the user will have the ability to assign the trailer to a specific yard location or to the Receiving Dock (if it is currently open, with nothing else scheduled there). If an appointment for this trailer's arrival had been associated with its receipt, then SCALE will default the Receiving Dock on the check in window. This is helpful so the guard does not have to make the decision of where to place the trailer. Once check in has been performed, users can bring up the Yard Check In/Out Window at any time to change/view the destination yard location or dock. This allows the yard jockey to see where to move the trailer, since the destination yard location will appear on their RF device during trailer moves.

 

 

 

Yard Jockey: RF Yard Management 

The worker (usually called a yard jockey) uses the RF device to facilitate the moving around of trailers in the yard. On the RF screens, the yard jockey will need to specify the trailer being moved, and where it is moving to. The yard jockey can move trailers between yard locations, or choose to move them directly to the Receiving Dock. Note that if someone previously entered a destination yard location (during check in, etc.), the system will default that location as the trailer's To Location, if the trailer is not already present in that location. If it is already in that location, then the system will default the Receiving Dock as the To Location.

 

 

 

Guard: Yard Check Out

Once a trailer has finished being processed on the Receiving Dock, it may be moved to a yard location for storage until it is ready to leave the yard. When leaving, the trailer needs to be checked out. Once again, the guard will use the SCALE full screen application to perform the check out action.
