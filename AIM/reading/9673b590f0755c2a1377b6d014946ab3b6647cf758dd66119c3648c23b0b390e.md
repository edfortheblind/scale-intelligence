
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1790" data-source-node="n57"></a>Wave Picking Group Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">During the pick group wave step, the system attempts to apply picking 
 groups to containers created during the container creation wave step. 
 A picking group determines how many loose or full containers can be included 
 in a group and the locations and/or work zones where the group can be 
 used. Picking groups are defined on the Picking 
 Group Window.</p>
