
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1656" data-source-node="n57"></a>Open Quantity Field</h1>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the amount of product 
 that you expect to receive into your warehouse. This quantity has not 
 been checked in. As soon as you begin checking in this receipt line, 
 the system decrements this amount accordingly.</p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n60">On the Work Insight Screen...</h5>
        <p data-source-node="n61">The open quantity represents the amount of unconfirmed quantity.</p>
