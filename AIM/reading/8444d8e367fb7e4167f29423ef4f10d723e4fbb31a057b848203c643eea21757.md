
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor623" data-source-node="n57"></a>Communication Method Buttons</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">These buttons determine when a message will be displayed on an RF device: 
 before the item is selected or after the item is selected. The communication method is associated with 
 text messages. These are defined on the Text Message Window.</p>
