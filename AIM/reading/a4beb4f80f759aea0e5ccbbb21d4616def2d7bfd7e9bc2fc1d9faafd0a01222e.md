
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1700" data-source-node="n57"></a>Result Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates at what state a container is at with regards to quality  control:</p>
        <ul data-source-node="n59">
            <li value="1" data-source-node="n60">Pending -QC has not yet been performed on the  container.<br data-source-node="n61"><br data-source-node="n62"></li>
            <li value="2" data-source-node="n63">Failed - QC has been performed on a container, and at least  one item quantity has failed the inspection.<br data-source-node="n64"><br data-source-node="n65"></li>
            <li value="3" data-source-node="n66">Passed - QC has been performed on a container, and all item  quantities have passed inspection. </li>
        </ul>
