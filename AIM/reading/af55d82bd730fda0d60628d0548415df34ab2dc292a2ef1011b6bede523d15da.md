
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor502" data-source-node="n57"></a>FedEx Rating Symbol Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the symbol that is used to identify a container 
 to the FedEx rating software. FedEx will use this value during rating 
 to help calculate the appropriate rates for containers associated with 
 this container type. For assistance on selecting or reading a rating symbol, 
 contact your system representative. This value is associated with a container 
 on the Container Type Window.</p>
