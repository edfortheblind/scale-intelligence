
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1805" data-source-node="n57"></a>Goal Rate Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field displays how much product the system expected the person to process in a minute. The system obtains the goal rate by performing the following calculation against the Labor Management Detail record:</p>
        <blockquote data-source-node="n59">
            <p data-source-node="n60">Goal Quantity / Total Actual Time = Goal Rate</p>
