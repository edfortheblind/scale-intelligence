
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1906" data-source-node="n57"></a>Catalog Number Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The catalog number associated with a particular 
 item. If you process catalog requests, use this field to identify the 
 item in the warehouse. All catalog numbers that are defined in the Item 
 Master or interfaced from the ERP are considered valid.</p>
