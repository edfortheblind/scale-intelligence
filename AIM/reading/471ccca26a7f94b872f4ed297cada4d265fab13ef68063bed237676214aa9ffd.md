
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1308" data-source-node="n57"></a>Reviewing Allocation Strategies</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">An allocation strategy is a methodology representing a series of algorithms 
 that the system employs to allocate product. For example, the strategy 
 FEFO allocates product based on the theory that product should be allocated 
 using the earliest expiration date. Note that if you want to use an 
 allocation strategy, keep in mind that if you specify "order by" 
 criteria on your allocation location selection record, the system will 
 use this "order by" criteria after the value(s) that the strategy 
 is meant to use. For example, if you select FEFO as your strategy (and 
 then also specify "order by" criteria), the system will use 
 your "order by" criteria after the expiration date in the sorting.</p>
