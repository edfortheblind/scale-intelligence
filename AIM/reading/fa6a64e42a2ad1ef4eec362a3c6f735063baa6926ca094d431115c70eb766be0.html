To Location Field

In Labor Management...

This field identifies the location to which you are transferring inventory using the Labor Activity Insight screen. The inventory is putaway at 'To Location'.

 

 
In Inventory Management... 

This field identifies the location to which you are transferring inventory using the Inventory Transfer screen.

 

 

In Work Execution...

This field identifies where product will be putaway during work execution.

 

 

In Replenishment... 

This field identifies the location (typically a forward picking location) that is eligible for replenishment because of diminished quantity.

 

 

In Immediate Needs Insight...

This field indicates the destination location of the immediate need.
