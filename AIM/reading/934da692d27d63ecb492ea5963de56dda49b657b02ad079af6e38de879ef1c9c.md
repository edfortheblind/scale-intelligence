
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1168" data-source-node="n57"></a>Label Master Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the label master record associated with a particular 
 wave master. A label master record manages the process of printing the 
 labels from a wave. The following labels are governed by these records: 
 shipping labels, vendor labels. Label masters are useful 
 because they allow you to define how/when these labels will be generated 
 and printed. You can also define which shipments the system will generate 
 labels for by creating label master criteria filter rules.</p>
