Mod 10 V3

This algorithm is used by the LTL carriers that use pro numbers as the unique shipment identifier, such as American Freightways.

  1. Take the numbers from the odd positions and multiple that number by 2: 02568 X 2 = 05136
     
  2. Take the results and add its individual numbers: 0 + 5 + 1 + 3 + 6 = 15
     
  3. Add the numbers from the even positions: 0 + 1 + 0 + 1 = 2
     
  4. Add the results from steps b and c: 15 + 2 = 17
     
  5. Subtract the result in step d from 100: 100 – 17 = 83
     
  6. Add two to the result from the last digit in step e:  3 + 2 = 5 (If this number is larger than 9 drop the first digit; for example, 10 would become 0)
     
  7. Multiple the base pro number by 10: 002150618 * 10 = 0021506180
     
  8. Add the result of step f to the result of step g: 0021506180 + 5 = 021506185
