        <p data-source-node="n58">Use this button to indicate that you want this dock location to be defined as a dock position. Dock positions are inventory-tracked dock locations in a dock area, and they represent a specific area inside a dock area. Note that a dock area may include any number of dock positions (including zero).</p>
