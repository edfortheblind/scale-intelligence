
        
        <h1 style="text-align: left;font-size: 12pt" data-source-node="n57">Freight Consolidator</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">A freight consolidator represents the place where a shipping load will delivered to, in lieu of its final destination. This configuration stores the address and contact information for each consolidator. Freight consolidators are used when generating a "consolidator" type master bill of lading. This address will display as the "Ship To Address" on the master BOL document. You can assign a consolidator to a load in the Shipping Load screen.</p>
