
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1911" data-source-node="n57"></a>Associating a Locating Rule with an Item</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">Locating rule can be associated with an item or a receipt line. You 
 can associate rules to receipt lines as you process them on the Receipt 
 Workbench. However, if you want a locating rule associated with 
 an item, you will need to complete this task on the Item Window during 
 setup.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
