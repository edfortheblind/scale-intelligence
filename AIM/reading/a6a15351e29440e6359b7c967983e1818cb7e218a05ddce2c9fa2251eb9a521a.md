
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor368" data-source-node="n57"></a>Status Field (Work Order)</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The status of a work order. Work orders can have the following statuses:</p>
        <blockquote data-source-node="n59">
            <p data-source-node="n60"><i data-source-node="n61">Scheduled</i> - The work order was 
 created, but not release.</p>
            <p data-source-node="n62"> </p>
            <p data-source-node="n63"><i data-source-node="n64">In Process</i> - The work order 
 was released, but not all of the finished items were confirmed.</p>
            <p style="font-style: italic" data-source-node="n65"> </p>
            <p class="p_1" style="font-style: italic" data-source-node="n66">Closed 
 - The work order was closed. Work orders can be closed at any point in 
 the process. </p>
        </blockquote>
