
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor805" data-source-node="n57"></a>Inspection Qty Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the actual amount/percentage you want the system 
 to set aside for quality control at a QC location.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">NOTE: Do not use a percentage 
 sign (%) to indicate percentages. For example, you would enter the value 
 20 percent as "20".</p>
