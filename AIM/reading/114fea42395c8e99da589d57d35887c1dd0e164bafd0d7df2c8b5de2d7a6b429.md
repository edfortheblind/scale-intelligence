
        
        <h1 style="text-align: left;font-size: 12pt" data-source-node="n57">Vendor</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n59">A vendor record identifies a company that supplies product to your warehouse. 
 You are not required to define vendor records, but they are helpful if 
 you need to contact a vendor concerning an particular receipt. You can 
 create parent and child vendor records. This is usually beneficial if 
 the parent record represents the corporate office and the child records 
 represent different distribution hubs.</p>
