
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor754" data-source-node="n57"></a>Shipping Load Status Fields</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">There are two shipping load status fields:</p>
        <ul data-source-node="n59">
            <li data-source-node="n60">The Leading Status field indicates the most progressed status of any shipment on the load. The shipment with the leading status is the one closest to completion.<br data-source-node="n61"><br data-source-node="n62"></li>
            <li data-source-node="n63">The Trailing Status field indicates the least progressed status of any shipment on the load. The shipment with the trailing status is the one furthest from completion.</li>
        </ul>
