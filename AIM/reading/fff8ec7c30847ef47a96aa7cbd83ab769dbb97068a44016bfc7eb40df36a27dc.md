
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1815" data-source-node="n57"></a>Functional Area Field</h1>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">A functional area represents a single or group of system modules that 
 performs an operational goal. Functional areas could refer to global warehouse 
 flows, such as Inbound or Outbound processing; or specific modules, such 
 as Receiving or Cycle Counting (among others).</p>
