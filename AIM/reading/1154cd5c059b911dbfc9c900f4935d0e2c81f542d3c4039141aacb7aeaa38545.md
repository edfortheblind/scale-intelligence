
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor639" data-source-node="n57"></a>Minimum Number of Lines Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies how many shipment 
 lines must be on a shipment for an email alert to be triggered. It works 
 in unison with the other fields on the Email Alerts Window: the criteria 
 defined in the window's other fields must also be met before the alert 
 can be triggered. </p>
