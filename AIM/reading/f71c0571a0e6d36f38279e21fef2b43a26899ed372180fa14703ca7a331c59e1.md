
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1292" data-source-node="n57"></a>Start Time Until End Time Fields</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the time span when an employee starts to work, 
 and when their shift ends. This span should equal the combined values 
 of the Hours Per Day and Break Hours Per Day Fields.</p>
