        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates what day and time a trailer is scheduled to arrive at the warehouse. </p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n61"><b style="color: #00008b" data-source-node="n62">Yard Management</b>
        </h5>
        <p data-source-node="n63">This value is defaulted from the appointment that was scheduled for this receipt.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n64"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n65"><b style="color: #00008b" data-source-node="n66">Appointment Calendar</b>
        </h5>
        <p data-source-node="n67">The date is displayed on the calendar screen. The date displayed depends on the view selected.</p>
        <ul data-source-node="n68">
            <li data-source-node="n69">Day view: A single calendar day appointments for all the dock doors of the warehouse is displayed. Click <b data-source-node="n70">&gt;</b> and <b data-source-node="n71">&lt;</b> to view the next and previous days appointment.</li>
            <li data-source-node="n72">7 day view: appointments for 7 day time line is displayed. The date is depicted for the span. For example: <i data-source-node="n73">June 10 - 17, 2019.</i></li>
        </ul>
