
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor322" data-source-node="n57"></a>Internal Load / Shipping Load Number Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">A system assigned number that represents a specific shipping load. A 
 shipping load represents all of the shipments assigned to a particular 
 carrier on a particular date. On a truck manifest, this field identifies 
 the number associated with the shipping load.</p>
