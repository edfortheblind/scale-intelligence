
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1521" data-source-node="n57"></a>Consolidated Checkbox or Toggle</h1>
        <h5 data-source-node="n58">For Shipments...</h5>
        <p data-source-node="n59">This checkbox/toggle indicates if two or more shipments 
 were consolidated. Shipments can be consolidated either in the ERP or 
 in the system.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n61"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n62">For Replenishment Requests...</h5>
        <p data-source-node="n63">This checkbox indicates that the system has combined this request with other(s) that share certain criteria. See the topic <a href="7baf040298fab232c96bdf940e75ea6c3249ac9941e375fb0f60ea2622c094a6.html" data-source-node="n64">Consolidate Replenishment Requests Checkbox</a> for more information on this criteria.</p>
