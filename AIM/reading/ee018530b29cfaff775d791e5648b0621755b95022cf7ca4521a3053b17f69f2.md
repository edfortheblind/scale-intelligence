
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1032" data-source-node="n57"></a>Carrier Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The carrier responsible for transporting the shipment(s) to your customer. 
 If this value is defined on the Receipt screen, it identifies the 
 carrier who transported product to you.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">All shipping loads are defined at the carrier level. All shipments on 
 a load must be associated with the same carrier. However, the shipments 
 may have different services. For example, a UPS shipping load can include 
 shipments with overnight, 2nd day, and ground services.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n61"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n62">To use rating, routing, and manifesting, you 
 will need to define each carrier on the Carrier Window.</p>
