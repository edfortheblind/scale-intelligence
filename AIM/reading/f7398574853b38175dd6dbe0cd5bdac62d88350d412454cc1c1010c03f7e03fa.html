Using the Shipment Monitoring: Customer Screen

You can use the Shipment Monitoring: Customer screen to research and manage waved shipments in a warehouse.

 

 

Notes
  • This topic discusses specific fields represented on this screen as it is shipped in the base SCALE product. If your screen has been customized, different field (s) may appear on your screen.

Navigating/Viewing the Inventory Monitoring Bar Chart...

  1. Access the Shipment Monitoring: Customer Screen.

  2. The system will display a chart on the first (highest) level which indicates the numbers of open shipments in the user's current warehouse broken down by Customer Category 1 (such as the value "Industry") from the shipment.

  3. Press on a Customer Category One data column, and the system will display the next level of the chart, Customer Name.

  4. Press on a Customer Name data column, and the system will then display the next level of the chart, Ship to.

  5. When you click on a Ship to column, the system will open the Shipment Insight screen with all the record(s) that meet the criteria you have chosen on the chart displayed on the Insight's data grid.

 

NOTES

  • On any level of the chart, clicking Insight takes you to Shipment Insight with the shipments selected that match that graph.

  • On each graph level, the totals at the top of the graph show number of open shipments, number of shipping loads tied to those shipments, and number of shipments with trailing status less than Ship Confirm Pending pertaining to that graph.

  • Changing warehouse at the top of the Monitoring screen displays the open shipments from the selected warehouse.

 

 

 

Summary Data...

The following summary data are accessible from the Shipment Monitoring: Customer screen:

 

Total Shipments

This indicates the number of shipments.

 

 

Total Shipping Loads

This indicates the number of unique shipping loads associated with the shipments being shown.

 

 

WIP Shipments

This indicates the shipments with trailing status less than Ship Confirm Pending.

 

 

Shipments Not Started

This indicates the shipments with the Picking Pending shipment status.

 

 

 

 

Indicator Tiles...

Indicator tiles display key warehouse data totals. Tapping on a tiles will navigate you to the appropriate insight screen that will load the data. The following indicator tiles are shipped with the system:

 

 

Loads Pending Confirm

This tile represents the number of shipping loads with a trailing status greater than or equal to Ship Confirm Pending and less than or equal to Load Confirm Pending. Pressing on this tile opens the Shipping Load Insight screen displaying these loads.

 

 

Future Loads

This tile indicates the number of shipping loads with a scheduled ship date greater than today. Pressing on this tile opens the Shipping Load Insight screen displaying these loads.

 

 

Shipments with No Carrier Assigned

This tile represents the number of shipments with no carrier assigned. Pressing on this tile opens the Shipment Insight displaying these shipments.
