Below is the calculation that the system uses for algorithm Mod 10 V5:

 

  1. If number is less than 10 characters, prefix a string of 0's to the value to obtain 10 characters. For example, if the number is 5645896, it becomes 0005645896.
     
  2. It number is greater than 10 characters, trim it to the rightmost 10 characters. For example, if the number is 289764923396, then trim it to 9764923396.
     
  3. Reverse the 10 characters.
    • 1st Example: 6985465000
    • 2nd Example: 6933294679

     

  4. Find the summation of all even digits.
    • 1st Example: 9+5+6+0+0=20
    • 2nd Example: 9+3+9+6+9=36

     

  5. Find the summation of all odd digits multiplied by 2.
    • 1st Example: 6+8+4+5+0=23*2=46
    • 2nd Example: 6+3+2+4+7=22*2=44

     

  6. Add the results of step 4 and 5.
    • 1st Example: 20+46=66
    • 2nd Example: 36+44=80

     

  7. Round off the result of step 6 to the nearest multiple of 10.
    • 1st Example: 70
    • 2nd Example: 80
