
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor109" data-source-node="n57"></a>Arrived Date Field</h1>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">The first day that at least one unit associated 
 with a receipt is checked in.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n60"><u style="font-style: italic" data-source-node="n61">Notes</u>
        </p>
        <ul data-source-node="n62">
            <li data-source-node="n63">The system blanks out this field if all receipt containers on the receipt are canceled from check in. <br data-source-node="n64"><br data-source-node="n65"></li>
            <li data-source-node="n66">This field is editable on the Receipt screen, so if you edit the field and then check product in, the value that you entered is retained.</li>
        </ul>
