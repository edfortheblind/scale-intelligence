
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor46" data-source-node="n57"></a>Number of Children Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the number of children 
 associated with a work instruction record. Parent and child records are generated during 
 work creation. The parent record identifies the work unit assigned during 
 work processing. A child record identifies one detail record that is used 
 to create that work unit.</p>
