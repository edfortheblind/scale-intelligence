
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1222" data-source-node="n57"></a>UPS World Ease Paperwork</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The following documents are related to the UPS World Ease Paperwork:</p>
        <ul type="disc" class="ul_1" data-source-node="n59">
            <li data-source-node="n60"><i data-source-node="n61">UPS World Ease 
 Box Label</i>: This label is affixed on the Document Box. It identifies 
 the hub that the World Ease group is being shipped to. <br data-source-node="n62"> </li>
            <li data-source-node="n63"><i data-source-node="n64">UPS World Ease 
 Consolidated Invoice:</i> This document summarizes the World Ease group. 
 It identifies the individual consignees (the final ship to destinations 
 beyond the hub), the number of containers per ship to destination, the 
 container tracking number(s), and the total cost/weight per consignee. 
 <br data-source-node="n65"> </li>
            <li data-source-node="n66"><i data-source-node="n67">UPS World Ease 
 Certificate of Origin:</i> This document is printed per shipment. It 
 is only printed if the final ship to destination is in a country that 
 requires a Certificate of Origin. <br data-source-node="n68"> </li>
            <li data-source-node="n69"><i data-source-node="n70">UPS World Ease 
 Master Invoice:</i> This document is printed per shipment. It details 
 what items are on the shipment and their value. </li>
        </ul>
        <p data-source-node="n71"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n72">For more information on the actual procedures for using this paperwork, 
 see the topic <a href="482ae1a88da6877dbd1d79b232796b16f27dfd3d13bbe9cdd8711be47743e126.html" data-source-node="n73">Processing UPS 
 World Ease Shipments</a>.</p>

        <h4 data-source-node="n77">Note On Rating Printer Stock Symbol...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n78">Where for printing with the label templates "TANDATA_UPS_WORLD_EASE_MAXICODE.STANDARD" 
 or "TANDATA_UPS_DOCBOX.STANDARD," set the Rating Printer Stock 
 Symbol on the Print Device Window  to 
 THERMAL_LABEL_UPS_WORLDEASE.</p>
