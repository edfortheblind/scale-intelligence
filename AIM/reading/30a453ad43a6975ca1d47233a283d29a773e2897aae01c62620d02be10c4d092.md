
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1709" data-source-node="n57"></a>Minimum Weight Break Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field is defined on the Weight Break configuration. It contains the weight used to determine the minimum charge break amounts used on the Rate Base configuration. For example, if the Minimum Weight Break amount field is defined at one hundred pounds and the weight of the shipment is eighty pounds, SCALE internal rating would use the Minimum Charge Less than Break field during freight charge comparisons. If the shipment weighed more than one hundred pounds, the system would use the Minimum Charge Greater Than or Equal To field during freight charge comparisons.</p>
