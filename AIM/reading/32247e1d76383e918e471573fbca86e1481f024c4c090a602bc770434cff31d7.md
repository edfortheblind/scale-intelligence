
        
        <h1 style="text-align: left" data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1689" data-source-node="n57"></a>Mandatory Status Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates if a status is required. 
 If a status is required, it must be included in all status flows associated 
 with a functional area.</p>
