
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor835" data-source-node="n57"></a>On Hand Quantity Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the item quantity that is  physically present at a location at that current moment. The location's on hand 
 quantity *does not diminish* until an item quantity is physically removed from a location. Note that this field will be blank by default. If you click OK button without entering any value, a validation error will occur indicating that the value is required. This functions the same way for full screen and RF. </p>
