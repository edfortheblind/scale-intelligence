
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1487" data-source-node="n57"></a>Pro/BOL Number Criteria Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field  indicates the filter record you want the system to use when  deciding which shipments on a wave to assign Pro/BOL numbers to. A Pro/BOL number is an identifier used by LTL carriers to uniquely identify a particular shipment.</p>
