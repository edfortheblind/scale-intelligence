
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1804" data-source-node="n57"></a>Carrier Service Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This value identifies a carrier's product 
 offering. Service examples include 1st day, 2nd day, and Ground. They 
 are typically used by parcel carriers. You can use this field to manage shipments 
 based on service.</p>
