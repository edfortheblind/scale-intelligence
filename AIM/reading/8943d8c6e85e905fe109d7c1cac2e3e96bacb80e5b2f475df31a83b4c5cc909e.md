
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor48" data-source-node="n57"></a>Sleep Time Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field defines the time intervals between 
 when a system summary service will process records. The service pauses 
 for this defined amount of time between record processing sessions. The 
 sleep time is expressed in minutes.</p>
