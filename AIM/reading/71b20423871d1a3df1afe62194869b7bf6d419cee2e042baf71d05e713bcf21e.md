        <p align="left" class="p_13" data-source-node="n57"> The company authorization section in Company Access tab enables you to authorize container types to companies. </p>
        <p align="left" class="p_13" data-source-node="n58"> </p>
        <p align="left" class="p_13" data-source-node="n59">when you select:</p>
        <p align="left" class="p_13" data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <ul data-source-node="n61">
            <li align="left" data-source-node="n62"><b data-source-node="n63">All</b> - The container type can be used for any of the companies.</li>
            <li align="left" data-source-node="n64"><b data-source-node="n65">List</b> - Authorize the container type for the selected companies.</li>
        </ul>
