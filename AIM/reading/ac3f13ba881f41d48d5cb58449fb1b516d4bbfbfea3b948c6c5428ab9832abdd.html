Reviewing Inventory Statuses

Inventory statuses indicate the status of items contained in a location. Two statuses are shipped with the system: available and unavailable. You may also create new statuses on this window. The system is shipped with the most common default values which should meet your business needs. The 'available to ship' and 'not available to ship' statuses will not have the 'system created' button selected. This allows you to delete them should your business not need them or use another type of status. Note that the system does not automatically look for a specific status when dealing with inventory. The system will allocate and ship held inventory if you do not set up your allocation rules to stop it.
