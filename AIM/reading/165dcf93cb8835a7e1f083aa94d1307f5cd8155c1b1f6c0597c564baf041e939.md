
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1984" data-source-node="n57"></a>Conditions Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field determines indicates the processing state of a putwall location sort process.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">The following are the conditions</p>
        <p data-source-node="n61"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n62">Sort Pending – Tote records that are eligible for sorted and that are not yet to be sorted.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n63"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n64">In Process – when user scans the tote id into the putwall location.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n65"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n66">Not Eligible – The tote is not eligible for being sorted. This happens when <b data-source-node="n67">Sort Picked Quantity</b> option is not selected on work profile.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n68"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n69">Sorted – Tote for which the sort is completed.</p>
