
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1257" data-source-node="n57"></a>ECCN Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The Export Commodity Classification Number Field determines 
 if the exporter must apply for an export license or if the exporter qualifies 
 for an exception based on the provisions of the Export Administration 
 Regulations. This is a five digit alphanumeric number that can be applied to all 
 products that are controlled by the Export Administration Regulations 
 (EAR). This value is required when the commodity is 
 shipped on an export license or a license symbol. It is issued by the 
 U.S. Government authorizing the export of specific commodities. </p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">You can find this field in the Shipment and Shipment Line Screens, and the fields on the shipment line are defaulted from the shipment.  This value also can be interfaced in a shipment download and upload. This information is passed when rating international shipments, and appears on the international documentation. When calling Progistics with shipping container details, the information will be retrieved from the associated shipment line.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n61"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n62">NOTE: Multiple ECCNs are not allowed on a shipment. </p>
