
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor2081" data-source-node="n57"></a>Re-rate at Ship Confirm Checkbox</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">If the carrier or service is set to system calculated freight charges and there are freight charges already associated with the shipment, this check box will determine whether the system will re-rate the shipment.  For parcel carriers rating at the container level, this would involve remanifesting the containers.  </p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">Note that if  the carrier or service is set to system calculated freight charges and there are no freight charges associated with the shipment, the shipment will be rated during ship confirm regardless of this setting.</p>
