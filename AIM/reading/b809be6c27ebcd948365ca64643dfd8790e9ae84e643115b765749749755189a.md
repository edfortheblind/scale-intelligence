
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1917" data-source-node="n57"></a>Putaway Zone Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the putaway zone associated 
 with a receipt line. This value can be interfaced from the ERP. 
 During locating, the system will only assign this receipt to a location 
 in the zone defined here.</p>
