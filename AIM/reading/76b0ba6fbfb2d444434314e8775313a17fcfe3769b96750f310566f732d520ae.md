
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1959" data-source-node="n57"></a>Item Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field represents the item that you either receive from a vendor 
 or want to ship to a customer. Items are usually defined on the Item Window 
 in the system, but they can also just be interfaced as needed. </p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n61">Component 
 Items...</h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n62">A component item is an item required to build a finished item. A component item 
 is defined on the Item Window or Item Info Accordion, just like any other item. Later, it is 
 identified as a component item on the Bill of Materials Window. </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n63"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n64"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n65">Note on Item Lookup Windows...</h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n66"> 

In the Item field of the Item Lookup screen, you can enter either an item ID or item cross reference value.</p>
