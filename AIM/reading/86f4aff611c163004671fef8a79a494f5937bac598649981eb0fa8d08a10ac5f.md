
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor412" data-source-node="n57"></a>Comments: Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The comments associated with a Message ID. 
 The comments are displayed on the RF device when the employee is processing 
 a work unit for a particular item/account/company. Generally, the comments 
 provide additional instructions for an item. This is especially helpful 
 when account requires special handling, such as adding seasonal stickers 
 to an item.</p>
