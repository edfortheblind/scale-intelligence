Creating/Viewing Receipt Appointments

You can use the system to schedule appointments for inbound receipts. Appointments provide the warehouse a way to schedule a certain receipt to arrive at the receiving dock. For example, suppose a certain carrier arrives at the warehouse at a specific time each week. You can then schedule an appointment for each of this carrier's receipts, so you can manage the flow through your receiving docks more efficiently. Appointments cannot be created after a receipt's items are checked in.

Creating an Appointment...

  1. Access a receipt on the Receipt Insight screen, and select the Schedule Appointment action. The system will display the Receiving Appointment Schedule screen.

  2. Use the Trailer ID Field to identify the truck trailer that will be arriving with product for your warehouse.

  3. Use the Receiving Dock Field to indicate from which dock this receipt's product will enter the warehouse. This typically would be the dock that the receipt's carrier regularly uses to bring product to the warehouse.

  4. Use the Start and End Date/Time Fields to indicate the day and time that you expect a certain trailer to arrive and leave the warehouse.

  5. Review the other appointment fields, and make changes as necessary. Note that if the leading status of the receipt is greater than check in pending, all the fields would be disabled.

  6. Press Save. The system will save your appointment.
