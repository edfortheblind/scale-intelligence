
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1724" data-source-node="n57"></a>Consignee Name and Address Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">These fields contains the name and address (street, city, state, and 
 zip) of the consignee. The consignee is the transportation company that 
 obtains your product from an international dock and delivers it to your 
 customer. Consignees are only used for international shipping when a secondary 
 transporter is need to move the product from a dock to the customer. They 
 are never used for domestic shipments. Generally, the consignee is interfaced on 
 a shipment from the ERP. However, you can add a consignee on the Shipment 
 Screen. </p>
