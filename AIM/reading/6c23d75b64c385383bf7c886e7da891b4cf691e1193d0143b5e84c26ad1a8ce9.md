
        
        <h1 style="text-align: left;font-size: 12pt" data-source-node="n57"><a name="kanchor1458" data-source-node="n58"></a>Load Data Selection</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n59">This field links a sequence on the document master to the appropriate data selection record. The data selection record determines which document type should be applied to a group of records in a wave. It also sorts the order that the paperwork is generated.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n61">This record is applied to shipping load records generated in a wave. It is used to generate truckload manifests.</p>
