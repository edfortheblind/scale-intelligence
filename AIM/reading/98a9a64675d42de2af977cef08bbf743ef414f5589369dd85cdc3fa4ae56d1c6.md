
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1566" data-source-node="n57"></a>QC Condition Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates at what state a container is at with regards to quality control:</p>
        <ul data-source-node="n59">
            <li data-source-node="n60"><i data-source-node="n61">Pending</i>: QC has not yet been performed on the container.<br data-source-node="n62"><br data-source-node="n63"></li>
            <li data-source-node="n64"><i data-source-node="n65">Failed</i>: QC has been performed on a container, and at least one item quantity has failed the inspection.<br data-source-node="n66"><br data-source-node="n67"></li>
            <li data-source-node="n68"><i data-source-node="n69">Passed</i>: QC has been performed on a container, and all item quantities have passed inspection.</li>
        </ul>
