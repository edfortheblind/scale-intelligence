Activating Inbound Quality Control

This setting will indicate whether or not the system will try to route a receipt's item quantities to quality control during the check in process. If you activate inbound quality control, the system will split off a portion of an item quantity, and send it to a designated QC location (which you can indicate using the Locating Rule Field on the Item Window). This assumes that the item has been made eligible for inbound quality control.

 

 

Procedures...

  1. Access the Receiving Preferences Window, open the appropriate preference record, and select the General Tab.
     
  2. Use the QC Inspection Active Checkbox to indicate that the system will route items to quality control during the check in process.
     
  3. Press OK The system will save your changes.
