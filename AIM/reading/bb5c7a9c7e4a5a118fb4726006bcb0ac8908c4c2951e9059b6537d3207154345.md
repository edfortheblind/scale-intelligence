
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1545" data-source-node="n57"></a>Locating Rule Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">A locating rule determines how 
 product is moved from the receiving dock to the appropriate warehouse location. </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n60">In Immediate Needs...</h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n61">When 
 processing an immediate need, locating rules are used in the fulfillment 
 process to determine where to locate the immediate need's requested quantity. The system uses the locating rule on the shipment line only when the receipt container does not cross dock, and it is not assigned to a put-to-store location (in the Store Location Assignment configuration).</p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n62"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n63"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n64">In 
 Inbound Quality Control...</h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n65">This field identifies a locating rule. When 
 the system routes an item quantity to a quality control location, locating 
 rules are used to determine where to locate the quantity being sent to 
 QC.</p>
