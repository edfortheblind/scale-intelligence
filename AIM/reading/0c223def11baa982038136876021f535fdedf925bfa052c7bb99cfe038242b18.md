
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56">Reprinting a FedEx Manifest</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n57">This procedure will allow you to reprint a FedEx manifest document. 
 The FedEx utility "Report/Revenue ReGenerator" provides the 
 ability to regenerate the revenue file produced after a ‘Close’ operation 
 or to reprint a set of ‘End of Day’ reports. </p>
        <p data-source-node="n58"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <h4 data-source-node="n60">Procedures...</h4>
        <ol class="ol_1" data-source-node="n61">
            <li value="1" data-source-node="n62">Select <i data-source-node="n63">Start 
 | Programs | FedEx Ship Manager Server | Utilities | ReGenerator</i>, 
 and the following window will be displayed:<br data-source-node="n64"><br data-source-node="n65"><img src="../source/assets/0a038af76da11a52ac9db33c95ef42b3977af0ad695a0ba4a57dccef780953ad.jpg" style="height: 306px;width: 552px" width="552" height="306" border="0" class="img_1" loading="eager" data-source-node="n66"><br data-source-node="n67"> </li>
            <li value="2" data-source-node="n68">Select the appropriate 
 manifest/invoice date for which you wish to generate the revenue 
 file or End-Of-Day reports.<br data-source-node="n69"><br data-source-node="n70">	-  To 
 generate the revenue files, click the Revenue Files button.<br data-source-node="n71"><br data-source-node="n72">	- To generate the End-Of-Day reports, 
 click the Close Reports button.  The 
 reports will be released to a printer or placed in a directory based on 
 the Meter configuration.<br data-source-node="n73"> </li>
            <li value="3" data-source-node="n74">Click Cancel 
 to close the application.</li>
        </ol>
