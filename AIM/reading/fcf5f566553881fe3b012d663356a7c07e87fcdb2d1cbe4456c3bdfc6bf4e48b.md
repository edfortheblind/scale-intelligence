
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor822" data-source-node="n57"></a>Percentage Toggle Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">When this field is specified in the Receipt From Purchase Order Screen, the screen takes the available quantity on each purchase order line times the percent, and rounds the result to the nearest whole number.  This becomes the receipt quantity on the line. If Percentage is toggled on, then Quantity is toggled off and vice versa. </p>
