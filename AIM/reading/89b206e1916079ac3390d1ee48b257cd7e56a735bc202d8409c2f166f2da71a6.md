
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1062" data-source-node="n57"></a>Mark For Address Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The shipping address of the Mark For customer. 
 A Mark For represents the final destination for a shipment, if it is different 
 from the Ship To address. You ship product to the Ship To address; another 
 party is responsible for transporting product from the Ship To address 
 to the Mark For address. Mark For addresses are commonly used by corporations 
 that want product delivered to a central distribution center, where it 
 will be further transported to individual Mark For retail stores.</p>
