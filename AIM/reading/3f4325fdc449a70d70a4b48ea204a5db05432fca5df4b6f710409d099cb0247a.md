
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1530" data-source-node="n57"></a>Assignment Method (Vocollect) Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field determines whether users can enter their own work ID when starting an assignment, or the work is automatically selected them. Either <i data-source-node="n59">system-directed</i> or <i data-source-node="n60">user-directed</i> is defaulted from the first detail record of the appropriate work profile record (the one associated with a Vocollect region). Note that the <i data-source-node="n61">group user</i> work assignment method is not supported.</p>
