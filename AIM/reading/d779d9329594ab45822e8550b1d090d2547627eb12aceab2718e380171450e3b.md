
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor2097" data-source-node="n57"></a>Meter Number Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies a unique shipping location for a FedEx account. 
 For example, if a company has warehouses in three different locations 
 (i.e. three shippers), then FedEx would provide them with three different 
 meter numbers.</p>
