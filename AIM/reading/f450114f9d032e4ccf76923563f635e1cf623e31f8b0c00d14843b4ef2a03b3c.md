
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1839" data-source-node="n57"></a>Priority Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The priority associated with a particular record. The lower the priority 
 number, the more likely that a record is selected. The system uses this 
 field to determine when it should select or process a record, based on 
 its priority in relation to the other records that need processing. For 
 example, a work instruction with the priority 10 will always be selected 
 before a work instruction with the priority 30.</p>
