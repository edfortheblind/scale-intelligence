
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor2016" data-source-node="n57"></a>Initial Yard Location Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the first place where a trailer will be dropped off, be it a yard location or the receiving dock. This field is required when performing yard check in. Note that once a trailer is checked in, this field's label changes to "Current Yard Location".</p>
