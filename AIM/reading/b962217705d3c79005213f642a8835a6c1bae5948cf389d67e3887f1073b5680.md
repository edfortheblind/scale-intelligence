
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor51" data-source-node="n57"></a>Success Display Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies which field (from the work instruction database table) that you want the system to display in your RF and Warehouse Mobile success message.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> 

WARNING: Keep in mind that you should choose an appropriate and useful field to display as a message on pick/putaway screens. If you choose an inappropriate value, the system may display irrelevant data (or no data at all), which could confuse the user.
</p>
