
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1209" data-source-node="n57"></a>Change Shipper Status Checkboxes</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This checkbox indicates if a carrier is registered 
 to ship for a particular shipper (or company). Progistics will only rate a carrier if its registered for the shipper. 
 Unregistered carriers are not rated. If a carrier is not registered to a shipper, 
 it will not be considered during carrier rating.</p>
