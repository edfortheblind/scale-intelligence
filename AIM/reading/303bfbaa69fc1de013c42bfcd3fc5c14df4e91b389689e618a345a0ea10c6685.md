
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor2038" data-source-node="n57"></a>Activating Inbound Quality Control</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This setting will indicate whether or not the system will try to route 
 a receipt's item quantities to quality control during the check in process. 
 If you activate inbound quality control, the system will split off a portion of 
 an item quantity, and send it to a designated QC location (which you can 
 indicate using the Locating Rule Field on the Item Window). This assumes 
 that the item has been made eligible for inbound quality 
 control.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <h4 data-source-node="n61">Procedures...</h4>
        <ol class="ol_1" data-source-node="n62">
            <li value="1" data-source-node="n63">Access the Receiving 
 Preferences Window, open the appropriate preference record, and 
 select the General Tab.<br data-source-node="n64"> </li>
            <li value="2" data-source-node="n65">Use the QC Inspection 
 Active Checkbox  to indicate that the system will route items to 
 quality control during the check in process.   
 <br data-source-node="n66"> </li>
            <li value="3" data-source-node="n67">Press OK 
 The system will save your changes.</li>
        </ol>
