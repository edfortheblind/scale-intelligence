
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor780" data-source-node="n57"></a>World Ease Status Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the status of a UPS World Ease group:</p>
        <ul type="disc" class="ul_1" data-source-node="n59">
            <li data-source-node="n60">Include Open Groups: 
 An open group can still have containers associated with it. Toggle On/Off as required.<br data-source-node="n61"> </li>
            <li data-source-node="n62">Show Closed Groups: 
 A closed group cannot have additional containers associated with it, and 
 cannot be reopened. Toggle On/Off as required. You must close a group in order to generate the appropriate 
 shipping paperwork. For more details on closed groups, see the topic   <a href="1a68f98c95372daccf6d14d7ee27d977d03e3f3d3306c249635fecde24aa9c10.html" data-source-node="n63">Processing UPS World Ease Groups</a>.</li>
        </ul>
