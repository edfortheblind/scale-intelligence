
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56">Active SCALE Data Retention Policy</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n57">The data retention period defines the total number of days (past the current date) that data will be kept online in SQL</p>
        <p data-source-node="n58">Databases. This data may be retained in the Primary DB Active tables (visible in SCALE and in SCI) or the Primary DB</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59">Archive Tables (visible in SCI). The data retention period for the tables mentioned above is 1 year (365 days). Data that</p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">has exceeded the retention period can be made available offline before being purged. The amount of data retained in</p>
        <p data-source-node="n61">the Primary DB Active tables, before being archived or purged (non-reportable data), will be managed by Manhattan to</p>
        <p data-source-node="n62">ensure optimal system performance.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n63"> </p>
        <ul data-source-node="n64">
            <li data-source-node="n65">
                <p data-source-node="n66">Not all data is available in SCI (Supply Chain Intelligence), certain data conditions or retrieval limits may apply</p>
            </li>
        </ul>
        <ul data-source-node="n67">
            <li data-source-node="n68">
                <p data-source-node="n69"> Non-reportable data will be flagged to be purged prior to the retention period.</p>
            </li>
        </ul>
