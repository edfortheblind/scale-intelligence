
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1042" data-source-node="n57"></a>Treat as Full Percent Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This value indicates the percentage of an item quantity that the system will consider a "full" unit of measure, IF the system is attempting to allocate the complete quantity for a location/license plate. If the quantity is above the defined Treat As Full Percent value, then the system will treat that quantity as the next higher unit of measure (e.g., a case or pallet).
</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">For example, suppose the following:</p>
        <ul data-source-node="n61">
            <li data-source-node="n62">The "full" quantity for the pallet unit of measure is defined as 100 EA<br data-source-node="n63"><br data-source-node="n64"></li>
            <li data-source-node="n65">The Treat as Full Percent setting is defined at 50%<br data-source-node="n66"><br data-source-node="n67"></li>
            <li data-source-node="n68">There is a quantity of 80 EA currently at the location</li>
        </ul>
        <p data-source-node="n69"> Therefore, an allocation of 80 EA would be considered a "full" pallet allocation.  However, an allocation of 79 EA would not. </p>
