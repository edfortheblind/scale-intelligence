
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1051" data-source-node="n57"></a>Transmit Status Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the status of the parcel manifest:</p>
        <ul data-source-node="n59">
            <li data-source-node="n60">
                <p data-source-node="n61"><i data-source-node="n62">Open</i> - The manifest was 
 created and containers are being associated with it. The manifest has 
 not been closed.</p>
                <p data-source-node="n63"> </p>
            </li>
            <li data-source-node="n64">
                <p data-source-node="n65"><i data-source-node="n66">Closed</i> - The manifest has 
 been closed. It cannot accept additional containers.</p>
                <p data-source-node="n67"> </p>
            </li>
            <li data-source-node="n68">
                <p data-source-node="n69"><i data-source-node="n70">Transmitted</i> - All manifest 
 information for this carrier was sent to its server. This status is valid 
 only if the carrier supports EDI transmissions.</p>
            </li>
        </ul>
