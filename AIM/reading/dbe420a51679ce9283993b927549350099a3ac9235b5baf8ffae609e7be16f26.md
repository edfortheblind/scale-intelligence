
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1734" data-source-node="n57"></a>Shipping Labor Planning Criteria Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">A shipping labor planning criteria record is used by the system to assign a criteria record to your labor group. This criteria includes rules that the system uses to determine what shipment(s) or work details a labor group will pick.</p>
