
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1261" data-source-node="n57"></a>Allocation Rejected Quantity Field</h1>
        <h5 data-source-node="n58"><b style="color: #000080;text-decoration: none" data-source-node="n59">On the Shipment Line Insight Screen...</b>
        </h5>
        <p data-source-node="n60">This field indicates the item quantity that has been rejected during the shipment allocation process. Keep in mind that the system updates this value for shipments whether or not they are flagged as allocate complete. Also, this field has a zero value when there are no rejections during shipment allocation. 
</p>
        <p data-source-node="n61"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n62"><i data-source-node="n63">Display Note:</i> Shipment details having an allocation rejection quantity value are represented by a pink icon.</p>
