
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor525" data-source-node="n57"></a>Override Field Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the shipping field value that you want the system 
 to override during a wave.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">When 'Override Created' flag is checked in the List Plane Grid Field of Inventory Insight, the icon in the inventory insight screen turns to pink color to indicate that override creation for the location. When the override is deleted for the location, there should be no icon.</p>
