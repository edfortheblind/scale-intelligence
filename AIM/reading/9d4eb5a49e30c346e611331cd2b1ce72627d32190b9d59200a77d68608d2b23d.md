
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1837" data-source-node="n57"></a>Total Container Quantity Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the total number of units 
 of all containers packed within a certain date range. For example, suppose 
 you have a container with one inner pack in it. The system considers this 
 1 in quantity, not the actual 
 quantity inside the inner pack (say 5).</p>
