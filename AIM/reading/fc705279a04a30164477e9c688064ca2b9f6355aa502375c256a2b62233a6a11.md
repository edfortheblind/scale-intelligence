
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor241" data-source-node="n57"></a>Generic Shipping Label</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">A generic shipping label displays the same type of information that 
 a regular <a href="0af121468add5373ba45862ad6c64cfae0f175760221a9f01a79812060e7d18b.html" data-source-node="n59">shipping label</a> does. The purpose 
 of this generic type of shipping label is to provide the option to print 
 a label regardless of what carrier you are using. Also, you can easily 
 change this label's field layout/design to conform to any carrier's specification. 
  </p>
