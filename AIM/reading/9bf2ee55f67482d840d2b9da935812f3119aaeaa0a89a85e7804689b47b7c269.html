Web User Field / Assigned Web User Field

A user who can access the Trading Partner Management module. The system references this field to identify a web user and provide them access to the system via the Internet. There are two types of web users: external and internal.

  • External Users are not employed by your organization. Common external users include customers, business partners and suppliers. These people need access only to their own shipments. You can allow these users to access one customer (via a customer ID) or a series of customers (via a customer group selection record).

  • Internal Users are employed by your organization.
