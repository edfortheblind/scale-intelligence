
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1534" data-source-node="n57"></a>Date Field</h1>
        <h5 data-source-node="n58">On the Shipping Calendar Window...</h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n59">This field identifies a no shipping day. You 
 can associate a carrier with a shipping calendar record. A shipping calendar 
 indicates which days a carrier will not transport product. You will use 
 the Date Field to define unique no ship days, such as holidays.</p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n61"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n62">On the Web Statistics Generation Window...</h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n63">This field identifies the day for which you 
 want to generate web statistics.</p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n64"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n65"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n66">On the Web Statistics Inquiry Windows...</h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n67">This field identifies the date range for your 
 statistics inquiry. The system will display statistics that fall in this 
 date range.</p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n68"> </p>
