
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1196" data-source-node="n57"></a>Total Two Step Errors Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the total number of a 
 cycle count plan's two step errors. These errors indicate that it took 
 "two steps" to fix a location's quantity discrepancy. You can 
 get this type of error when the system creates work and there is a discrepancy 
 at the location, and the user cannot reconcile the location based on their 
 preferences.</p>
