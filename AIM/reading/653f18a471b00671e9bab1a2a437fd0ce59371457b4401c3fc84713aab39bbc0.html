Email Address Field

This field indicates the email address associated with a particular entity (such as a user, vendor, etc).

 

 

On the Warehouse Alerts Window...

You can use this field to identify where you will send a warehouse alert email message:

  • Select the Email Address Field to define the address that the system will send the alert to (for example, customer@store.com).
     
  • Select the Ship From Email Address Button to send the alert to the email address defined on the Ship From Tab of the Receipt Screen.
     
  • Select the Vendor Email Address Button to send the alert to the email address defined on the Source Tab of the Receipt Screen.
     
  • Select the Alternate Email Address Button to send the alert to the email address defined on the General Tab of the Shipment Screen.
