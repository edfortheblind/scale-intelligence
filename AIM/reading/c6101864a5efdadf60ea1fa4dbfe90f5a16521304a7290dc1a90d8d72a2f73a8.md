
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1786" data-source-node="n57"></a>Upload Closed Receipts with 0 Quantity Received Checkbox</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">If  this field is selected and the Close Date value on the receipt is entered, all closed receipts with zero quantity for the matching company will be uploaded.  A closed receipt is a receipt that has been manually closed before any receipt containers have been checked in. </p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">The Upload Closed Receipt Details With 0 Quantity Received field will be used as the receipt header for companies that match the company configuration.  If the receipts match the company configuration and this field is selected, details of the closed receipts with 0 quantity received will be uploaded.  If this field is not selected, the receipt details will not be uploaded even if the receipts are matching the company configuration.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n61"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n62"> 
For receipt headers without a company, the warehouse configuration is used . If the receipts match the warehouse configuration and this field is selected, details of the closed receipts with 0 quantity received will be uploaded.   If this field is not selected, the receipt details will not be uploaded even if the receipts are matching the warehouse configuration.
</p>
        <p data-source-node="n63"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n64">If both the Upload Closed Receipts with 0 Quantity Received and Upload Receipt Details with 0 Quantity Received fields are selected for the Company or Warehouse configuration, then the interface will upload both the receipt header and the receipt lines. Otherwise, only the receipt header will be uploaded.</p>
