
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1548" data-source-node="n57"></a>Document Template Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the document template associated with a document. 
 The system uses document templates to define the structure of a document. 
 All paperwork documents generated by the 
 system are associated with SQL Server Reporting Services templates (.rdl). All documents generated by Progistics or 
 FedEx are associated with a template that you cannot access or modify.</p>
