
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1099" data-source-node="n57"></a>Comment Field</h1>
        <h5 data-source-node="n58">On the Wave Screen...</h5>
        <p data-source-node="n59">This field identifies why a wave failed a particular wave step. Generally, 
 a wave step fails due to a configuration problem. You cannot release a failed wave. Instead, you must cancel the wave 
 and assign the shipments to new waves.</p>
