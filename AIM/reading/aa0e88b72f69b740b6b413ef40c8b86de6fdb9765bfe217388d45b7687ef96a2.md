
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1344" data-source-node="n57"></a>Suspense Quantity Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the inventory standing 
 of item(s) in a specific location. The "suspended" status is 
 used during cycle counting, when the quantity counted does not equal the 
 quantity the system has recorded.</p>
