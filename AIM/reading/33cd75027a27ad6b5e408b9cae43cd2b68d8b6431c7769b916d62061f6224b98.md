
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56">All Inventory In Warehouse Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n57">For a Company Transfer action, this field indicates that you want to 
 transfer a warehouse's entire quantity of this item. <a name="kanchor313" data-source-node="n58"></a>Note that company transfers can 
 only be performed on locations where their entire quantity is in an Available 
 or  On Hand 
 status. You cannot perform a company transfer on a location where any 
 quantity is in an Allocated, In Transit and/or Suspended status. You cannot 
 perform a company transfer on product at the shipping dock, the receiving 
 dock, or the receiving "pre-check in" location. Also the company transfer will happen only if the item master have multiple item/company records.</p>
