
        
        <h1 style="text-align: left;font-size: 12pt" data-source-node="n57"><a name="kanchor154" data-source-node="n58"></a>Work Group</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n59">A Work Group categorizes similar activities that help move products through the warehouse. Examples include Receiving, Picking, Packing, and Shipping. Each work group can encompass multiple related Work Types.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">For instance, “Order Picking” and “Batch Picking” are work types under the broader “Picking” work group.</p>
