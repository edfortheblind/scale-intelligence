Planned Ship Date Field

This field indicates the original scheduled ship date that was interfaced from the ERP. This value is not updated by the system.

 

 

Notes...
  • When the scheduled ship date is interfaced from the ERP, the system defaults this value in the Planned Ship Date Field.

  • When a shipment is copied or split, the Planned Ship Date of the original shipment is used on the resulting new shipment.
