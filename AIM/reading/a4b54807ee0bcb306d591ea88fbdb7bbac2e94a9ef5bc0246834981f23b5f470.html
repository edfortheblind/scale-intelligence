Activate Rejected Quantity Window

You can use this screen to move a portion (or all) of a shipment's quantity out of a "rejected" status and into a status between "picking pending" and "ship confirm pending." You can access this screen from the shipment line level. For more information on this process, please see the topic Shipment Processing.
