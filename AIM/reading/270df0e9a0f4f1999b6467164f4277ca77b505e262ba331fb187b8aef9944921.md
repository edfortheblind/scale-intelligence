
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor381" data-source-node="n57"></a>Rating Profile Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field is displayed on the Carrier Window. A rating profile estimates 
 how many containers are on a shipment. When rating is run during a wave, 
 physical containers have not been created yet. To obtain a rate estimate, 
 the system references the rating profile record. The rating profile also determines if the 
 system will re-rate a shipment at ship confirmation. Generally, containers 
 are rated during the wave and each time they are manifested. However, 
 the system can re-rate at ship confirmation. </p>
