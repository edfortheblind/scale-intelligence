
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1514" data-source-node="n57"></a>Number of Full Containers Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field determines how many full containers can fit in a picking 
 group. A container is considered 'full' if its entire quantity is shipped 
 to a customer and no additional items are packed into it. Generally, full 
 cases are shipped in the storage container. This value is defined on the Picking Group 
 Window. The system references it during the pick group creation wave step.</p>
