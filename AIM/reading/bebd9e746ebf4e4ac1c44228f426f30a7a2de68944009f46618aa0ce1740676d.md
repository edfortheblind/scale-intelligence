
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor415" data-source-node="n57"></a>Packing Category Field</h1>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">The packing category associated with a particular 
 item. A packing category indicates which items can 
 share a shipping container. Generally, like-sized and like-shaped items 
 share a packing category. In addition, 
 they can be used to associate items with unique shipping needs, 
 such as fragile or hazardous items.</p>
