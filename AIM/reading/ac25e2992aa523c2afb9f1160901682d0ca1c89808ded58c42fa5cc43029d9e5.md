
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1065" data-source-node="n57"></a>Allow Nest After Putaway Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates to the system that you want the nest after putaway screen to display (upon delivery to a To Dock Location) when putting away shipping containers. Note that the Nest After Putaway Checkbox on the Work Profile Window must also be selected to use this functionality.</p>
