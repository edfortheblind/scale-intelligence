
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1033" data-source-node="n57"></a>Compute Delivery Date Checkbox</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">Use this checkbox to indicate if you want the system to automatically 
 calculate the anticipated delivery date of a shipment using the shipment's 
 "days to deliver" data. For example, suppose you have a shipment that has a "scheduled 
 to ship" date of Monday, and the shipment should take two days to 
 arrive. Therefore, if this checkbox is selected, the system will calculate 
 that the shipment should arrive at its destination on Wednesday, and populate 
 the appropriate delivery date field on the shipment. </p>
