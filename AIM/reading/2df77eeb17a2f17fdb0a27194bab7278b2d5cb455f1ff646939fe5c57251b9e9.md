
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor839" data-source-node="n57"></a>Nest After Putaway Checkbox</h1>
        <h5 data-source-node="n58">On 
 the Work Profile Detail Window...</h5>
        <p data-source-node="n59">This checkbox is used in RF group picking, when you are picking into 
 a final shipping container (only when the work unit is for a single shipment). 
 If you check this checkbox, the system will display a nesting RF screen 
 after you have putaway your shipping containers. If you specify a container, 
 the picked containers are then nested under it. This allows you to do 
 is pick into shipping containers, and then place those shipping containers 
 on a pallet for shipping. Note that the nest after putaway functionality described above is not supported when performing putaway to shipping container.</p>
