
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor140" data-source-node="n57"></a>Consolidator ID Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field represents the place where a shipping load will delivered 
 to, in lieu of its final destination. At this consolidator, the particular 
 shipments of a load will be organized according to where they are to be 
 delivered.</p>
