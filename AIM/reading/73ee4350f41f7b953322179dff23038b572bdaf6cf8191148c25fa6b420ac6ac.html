Freight Consolidator

A freight consolidator represents the place where a shipping load will delivered to, in lieu of its final destination. This configuration stores the address and contact information for each consolidator. Freight consolidators are used when generating a "consolidator" type master bill of lading. This address will display as the "Ship To Address" on the master BOL document. You can assign a consolidator to a load in the Shipping Load screen.

 

Add New Freight Consolidator

 

Use the Address Fields to provide the location of your consolidator.
