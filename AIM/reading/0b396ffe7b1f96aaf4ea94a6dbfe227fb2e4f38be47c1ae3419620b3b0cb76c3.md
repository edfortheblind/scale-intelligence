
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor150" data-source-node="n57"></a>Less than 'X' Weight Break Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">These fields represents the charges per LTL class for each shipment weighing less than the defined ('X') weight. The actual amounts are defined on the 'Rating Weight Break' configuration. These values are used on the Rate Base record. To calculate the freight charge, the system looks at the LTL class, then takes the weight of the shipment divided by 100 and multiplies it by the rate defined here. </p>
