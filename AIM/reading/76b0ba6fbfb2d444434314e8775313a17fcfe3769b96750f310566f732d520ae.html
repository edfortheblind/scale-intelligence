Item Field

This field represents the item that you either receive from a vendor or want to ship to a customer. Items are usually defined on the Item Window in the system, but they can also just be interfaced as needed.

 

 

Component Items...

A component item is an item required to build a finished item. A component item is defined on the Item Window or Item Info Accordion, just like any other item. Later, it is identified as a component item on the Bill of Materials Window.

 

 

Note on Item Lookup Windows...

In the Item field of the Item Lookup screen, you can enter either an item ID or item cross reference value.
