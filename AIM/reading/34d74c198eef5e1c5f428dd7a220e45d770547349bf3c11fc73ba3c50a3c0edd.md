
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1233" data-source-node="n57"></a>Creating Custom Status Flows</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">A custom status flow accommodates the processing needs of special or 
 unique items. Custom flows are defined per functional area. They cannot 
 be shared across functional areas: in other words, a custom flow associated 
 with the outbound functional area cannot also be used by the inbound functional 
 area.</p>

        <h5 data-source-node="n96">Using the Header Fields</h5>
        <ol class="ol_1" data-source-node="n97">
            <li value="1" data-source-node="n98">Use the Flow 
 Name Field to provide an identifier for your custom status flow. 
 Also provide a description.<br data-source-node="n99"> </li>
            <li value="2" data-source-node="n100">Use the Functional 
 Area Field to indicate if your flow will be Outbound (shipment 
 processing) or Inbound (receipt processing).Z</li>
        </ol>
        <p data-source-node="n101"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n102">Using the Detail Records Tab</h5>
        <ol class="ol_1" data-source-node="n103">
            <li value="1" data-source-node="n104">Use the Status 
 Name Checkbox to include a status in the flow. You create the steps 
 of the status flow by selecting its statuses from this tab. All mandatory 
 statuses are checked. They are automatically included in this new flow 
 and cannot be deselected. If you select one with a related status, the 
 system will include both of these statuses in the flow.</li>
        </ol>
