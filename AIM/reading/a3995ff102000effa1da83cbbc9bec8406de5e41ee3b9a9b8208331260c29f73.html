Searching/Viewing Multiple Order Pallet Records...

  1. Open the Multiple Order Pallet Insight Screen.

  2. Use the Basic Criteria Fields (located on the Filter Pane on the left side of the screen) to indicate the values for your primary search:

  3. Press the Apply Filter Button. The system will display the resulting records in the List Pane.
