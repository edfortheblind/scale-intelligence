
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor879" data-source-node="n57"></a>Using the Receipt Quality History Insight Screen</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">You can use the Receipt Quality History Insight screen to search and manage receipt quality records in your warehouse. Any time you check in a license plate on a new receipt, close a receipt, or reopen a closed receipt, the system writes a quality record with reason code. Examples of receipt quality history reason codes are Items not received, Receipt received on time, and Missed expected receipt date.</p>

        <h4 data-source-node="n229"><a name="Searching" data-source-node="n230"></a>Searching/Viewing Receipt Quality History Records...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n231">You can use the Receipt Quality History Insight Screen to search for and view receipt quality records in your warehouse. </p>
        <ol data-source-node="n232">
            <li value="1" data-source-node="n233">Open the Receipt Quality History Insight Screen.<br data-source-node="n234"><br data-source-node="n235"></li>
            <li value="2" data-source-node="n236">Use the Basic Criteria Fields (located on the Filter Pane) to indicate the values for your primary search.<br data-source-node="n237"><br data-source-node="n238"></li>
            <li value="3" data-source-node="n239">Press the Apply Filter Button. The system will display  the resulting records in the List Pane. <br data-source-node="n240"><br data-source-node="n241"></li>
            <li value="4" data-source-node="n242">If you select one of the result records from the List Pane, then the system will display its information in the Detail Pane.</li>
        </ol>
