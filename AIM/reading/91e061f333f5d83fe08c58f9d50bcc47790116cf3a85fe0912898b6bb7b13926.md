
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1755" data-source-node="n57"></a>Instructions / Total Instructions Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The total number of instructions associated with a work unit, work order, 
 or a condition. When instructions are associated with 
 a work unit, they are defined on the Bill of Materials Window and 
 are printed on work order paperwork. Employees will use these instructions 
 to determine how to assemble a finished item.</p>
