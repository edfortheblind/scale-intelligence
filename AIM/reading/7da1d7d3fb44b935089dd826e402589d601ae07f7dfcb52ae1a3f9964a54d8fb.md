
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor709" data-source-node="n57"></a>Current Location Field</h1>
        <h5 data-source-node="n58">Using Work Orders...</h5>
        <p data-source-node="n59">This field appears on the Finished Item Breakdown 
 screen. It identifies the storage location associated with the finished 
 item that you want to disassemble.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n61"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n62">Using Yard Management...</h5>
        <p data-source-node="n63">This field indicates the yard location that a trailer currently resides at.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n64"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n65"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n66">On the Shipping Container Screen...</h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n67">Note that this field can include P&amp;D locations when confirming RF shipment and dock management work. Also keep in mind that RF shipment work uses the equipment location (in contrast to the RF dock management work).</p>
