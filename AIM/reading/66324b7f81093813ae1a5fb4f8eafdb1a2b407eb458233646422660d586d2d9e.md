
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1396" data-source-node="n57"></a>Device Type Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">You can use this field to indicate what type(s) of devices you want the Labor Activity Insight to display records for in the List Pane Grid Fields: </p>
        <ul data-source-node="n59">
            <li data-source-node="n60"><i data-source-node="n61"> Full Screen:</i> Choose this option to see labor records that originated from a full-screen source, such as an explorer or workbench window.<br data-source-node="n62"><br data-source-node="n63"></li>
            <li data-source-node="n64"><i data-source-node="n65">RF:</i> Choose this option to see labor records that originated from an RF-screen source.<br data-source-node="n66"><br data-source-node="n67"></li>
            <li data-source-node="n68"><i data-source-node="n69">External:</i> Choose this option to see labor records that originated from an external source, such as a web service.</li>
        </ul>
