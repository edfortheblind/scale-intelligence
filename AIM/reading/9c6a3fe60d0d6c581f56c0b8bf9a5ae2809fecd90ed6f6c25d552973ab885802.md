
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor470" data-source-node="n57"></a>Shipping Calendar Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58"> This field is used to associate a carrier with a shipping calendar record. A shipping 
 calendar identifies the days when a carrier cannot transport product. 
 You can define weekly no ship days and unique no ship days, such as holidays.</p>
