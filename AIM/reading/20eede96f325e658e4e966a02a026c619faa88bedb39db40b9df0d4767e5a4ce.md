
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor898" data-source-node="n57"></a>Number of Scans Allowed Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the number of barcodes that the user will be required 
 to scan on a GS1 label. The system will expect this number of scans during 
 GS1 RF receipt check in.</p>
