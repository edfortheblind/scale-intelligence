
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor815" data-source-node="n57"></a>Container Pack List</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">A container pack list identifies the items packed in a specific container(s). 
 It also includes the customer's address and other general shipping information. 
 You can obtain this report at the shipment level or the container level. 
 The shipment level identifies all containers and contents associated with 
 that shipment. Packing lists can be automatically generated when a container 
 is packed and/or unpacked. You can manually generate them from the Packing 
 Screen. </p>
