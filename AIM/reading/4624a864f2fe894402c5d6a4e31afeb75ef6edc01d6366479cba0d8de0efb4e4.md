
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1176" data-source-node="n57"></a>Volume UM Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the unit of measure that your company uses 
 to determine volume. You can use any unit of measure. However, 
 this value must apply to all items, containers, shipments and shipping 
 loads defined in the warehouse.</p>
