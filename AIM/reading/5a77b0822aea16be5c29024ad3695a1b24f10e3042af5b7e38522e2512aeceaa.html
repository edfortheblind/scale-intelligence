Upload Receipt Details With 0 Quantity Received Checkbox

If this checkbox is selected and the Close Date value on the receipt is entered, the receipt lines with zero quantity for the matching company will be uploaded. A receipt with a closed date indicates receiving has completed against this receipt. A receipt line has 0 quantity received when the difference between the total quantity and the open quantity is zero.

 

The Upload Receipt Details With 0 Quantity Received field will be used as the receipt header for companies that match the company configuration. If the receipts match the company configuration and this checkbox is selected, details of the receipt with 0 quantity received will be uploaded. If it is not selected, the receipt details will not be uploaded even if the receipts are matching the company configuration.

 

For receipt headers without a company, the warehouse configuration is used . If the receipts match the warehouse configuration and this checkbox is selected, details of the receipt with 0 quantity received will be uploaded. If it is not selected, the receipt details will not be uploaded even if the receipts are matching the warehouse configuration.

 

When a receipt is partially received and uploaded, and then closed manually, the closure is uploaded only if this checkbox is selected.
