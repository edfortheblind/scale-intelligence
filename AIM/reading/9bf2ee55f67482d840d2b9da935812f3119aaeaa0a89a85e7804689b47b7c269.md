
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1939" data-source-node="n57"></a>Web User Field / Assigned Web User Field</h1>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">A user who can access the Trading Partner 
 Management module. The system references this field to identify a web 
 user and provide them access to the system via the Internet. There are 
 two types of web users: external and internal. </p>
        <ul data-source-node="n59">
            <li data-source-node="n60"><i data-source-node="n61">External 
 Users</i> are not employed by your organization. Common external users 
 include customers, business partners and suppliers. These people need 
 access only to their own shipments. You can allow these users to access 
 one customer (via a customer ID) or a series of customers (via a customer 
 group selection record).<br data-source-node="n62"><br data-source-node="n63"></li>
            <li data-source-node="n64"><i data-source-node="n65">Internal 
 Users</i> are employed by your organization.</li>
        </ul>
