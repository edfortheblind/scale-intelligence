        <h4 data-source-node="n65">Summary...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n66">The paperwork process includes the generation and printing of paper documents and labels. You can obtain paperwork for all 
 phases of the system's outbound and inbound processes, including such 
 key processing points as running a wave, closing a container, or confirming 
 a shipment/load. You can configure SCALE to use several different applications to render/print documents/labels, including SQL Server Reporting Services or Progistics. Keep in 
 mind that if the shipped document templates do not meet your business 
 needs, you can easily create custom documents by editing the 
 template files. This allows you to meet a vendor's specific 
 document requirements.</p>

        <h4 data-source-node="n83">Custom Documents...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n84">A number of predefined documents and labels are shipped with the application, such as pick lists, bills of lading, and container labels, just to name a few. Keep in mind that you can also create your own documents by copying a predefined 
 template file and modifying it; or, you can create a completely new template. Document templates are stored in the Printing directory on your application 
 server. Note that any custom templates that you create must also be stored 
 in this directory.</p>

        <h4 data-source-node="n88">Paperwork and Progistics...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n89">Progistics generates certain parcel manifests and shipping container labels. Note that Progistics uses its own templates which cannot be modified.</p>
