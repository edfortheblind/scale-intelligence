
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor756" data-source-node="n57"></a>Bundle ID Field</h1>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">A Progistics generated number assigned to 
 containers being shipped within the continental Americas, but not in the 
 domestic USA. Progistics generates this value during manifesting. This 
 value is used by Progistics to generate commercial invoices and shipping 
 labels.</p>
