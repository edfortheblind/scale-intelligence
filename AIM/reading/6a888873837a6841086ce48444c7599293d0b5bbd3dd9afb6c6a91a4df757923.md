
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1625" data-source-node="n57"></a>Immediate Dock Transfer Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the work type that the system will associate with immediate dock transfers (manual transfers, without work). The system will use this work type for the writing of transaction history and labor management details when manual transfers of containers are performed.</p>
