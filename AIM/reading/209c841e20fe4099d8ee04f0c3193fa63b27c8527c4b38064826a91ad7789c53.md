
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1288" data-source-node="n57"></a>Work Unit Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the work unit associated 
 with a work instruction. Work units are assigned by either the system 
 or employees, depending on the work profile used to generate the instruction. From the Work Insight Screen, clicking on the Work unit in the header opens the Work Detail screen for the selected instruction.</p>
