
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1093" data-source-node="n57"></a>Include In Item Balance Upload Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates that you want to upload item balance data for items 
 that are in a particular location class. When this field is inactive, 
 the inventory in all locations of this location class will not be included 
 in the uploaded data.</p>
