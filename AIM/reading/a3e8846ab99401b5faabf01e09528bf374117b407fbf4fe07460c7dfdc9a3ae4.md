
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56">Calculate Time Based On Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n57">This field  indicates by what entity that you want the labor plan execution wave step to calculate this group's time data:</p>
        <ul data-source-node="n58">
            <li data-source-node="n59"><i data-source-node="n60">Unit of Measure</i>: The system will calculate the estimated time data based on <a name="kanchor1722" data-source-node="n61"></a>the UM that the worker will be picking. For an example of this calculation, see the "Scenarios" section of the <a href="7dd4c71c4fffda7e9ee1126c5142b92f5d6ebf82faf4a4f204c59dfe5f1bfd65.html" data-source-node="n62">Labor Management Process Summary</a> topic.<br data-source-node="n63"><br data-source-node="n64"></li>
            <li data-source-node="n65"><i data-source-node="n66">Quantity</i>: The system will calculate the estimated time based on the quantity of the item that the worker will be picking. For an example of this calculation, see the "Scenarios" section of the <a href="7dd4c71c4fffda7e9ee1126c5142b92f5d6ebf82faf4a4f204c59dfe5f1bfd65.html" data-source-node="n67">Labor Management Process Summary</a> topic.</li>
        </ul>
