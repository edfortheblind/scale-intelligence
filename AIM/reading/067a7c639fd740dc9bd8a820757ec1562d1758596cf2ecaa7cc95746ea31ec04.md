
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor787" data-source-node="n57"></a>Fill Percent Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field appears on the Container Group Detail Window. You will use 
 it to identify the percentage of a container's volume that is available 
 for storing items. If the container includes packing materials, such as 
 bubble-wrap, you should 'deduct' that material's volume from the fill 
 percentage. For example, if packing materials require 10% of the container's 
 total volume, the fill percent would be 90% (assuming that only the packing 
 materials and the items are shipped in the container).</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n60">During the container creation wave step, the 
 system references this value when selecting a container for a loose each 
 record. </p>
