
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor549" data-source-node="n57"></a>Include Freight Charge In Amount Checkbox</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This checkbox indicates how the system will send the freight charge 
 of a container to Progistics. If this check box is selected, the system 
 will make two "calls" to Progistics. The first call will retrieve 
 the freight charge from Progistics. The second will pass the container 
 amount (including the freight charge) in as the amount for manifesting. </p>
