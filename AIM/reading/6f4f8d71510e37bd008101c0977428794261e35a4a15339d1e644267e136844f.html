Container Type Field

A container type identifies the type of package that inventory is either checked in during receiving or packed in for shipping. Container types are usually defined by their dimensions. Create a record for each container type that your warehouse uses during check in or packing.

 

 

On the Packing Screen section...

- The container types available for packing can be authorized by company and warehouse.

 

On the Single Unit Packing Screen section...

- The container types available for packing can be authorized by company and warehouse.

 

On the Shipping Container Screen section...

- The container types available for shipping container can be authorized by company and warehouse.

 

On the RF Container Type Screen section...

- The container types available for picking and putaway into shipping container can be authorized by company and warehouse.

 

On the Receipt Workbench Screen section

- The container types can be authorized by company and warehouse while creating a new parent receipt container from the Receipt container screen.
