
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1053" data-source-node="n57"></a>FTZ Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the foreign trade zone number (FTZ) where the 
 cargo is stored before leaving U.S. for exportation. A foreign trade zone 
 is an area designated by the government for duty free entry of any non-prohibited 
 goods. This value applies to international shipments 
 and appears on the Shipper's Export Declaration Form. </p>
