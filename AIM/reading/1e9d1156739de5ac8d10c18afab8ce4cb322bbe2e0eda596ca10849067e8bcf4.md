
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor2064" data-source-node="n57"></a>Truck Manifest</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">A truck manifest identifies each shipment on a truck, including its 
 weight, freight terms and address. In addition to shipment information, 
 the truck manifest provides load information, such as the carrier, carrier 
 type (truckload or LTL) and ship on date.</p>
