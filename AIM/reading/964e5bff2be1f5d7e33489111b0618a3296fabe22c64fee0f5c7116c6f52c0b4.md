        <p data-source-node="n58">Pallet building criteria records indicates how the wave process will decide which shipping containers are eligible to add to a newly-constructed pallet. This criteria also determines how the containers are grouped onto pallets (for instance, having different item category's placed onto separate pallets).</p>
