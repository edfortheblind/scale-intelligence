
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor461" data-source-node="n57"></a>Build Level Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This is an optional field on the Bill of Materials Window. If you are 
 building a finished item in stages, or levels, you can use this field 
 to identify which components are required for a certain stage. Usually, 
 information defined on the Instruction Tab of Bill of Materials Window 
 descibe how to use the items retrieved for a build level. </p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">For example, suppose you are assembling a bicyle in two stages. During 
 stage one, you need to assemble the tires; during stage two, you would 
 assemble the entire bike. In this case, all of the components required 
 to assemble the tires, the frames, tubes and tires, are assigned the build 
 level 1. The other bike components, such as the brakes and the seat, are 
 assigned the build level 2. </p>
        <p data-source-node="n61"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n62">Build levels are very flexible. Your organization 
 can use them in whatever manner best fits your needs. </p>
        <p data-source-node="n63"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n64"> In the Work Order Line Details screen, you can find the same field. </p>
