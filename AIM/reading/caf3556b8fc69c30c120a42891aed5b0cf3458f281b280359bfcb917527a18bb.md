
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor38" data-source-node="n57"></a>Consolidation After Putaway Checkbox</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This option identifies if you want to invoke the consolidation putaway process after performing a work putaway action on an RF device. Note that if the Automatic Putaway setting is selected, the Consolidation After Putaway setting will not be available to select.</p>
