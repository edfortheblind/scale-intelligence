
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor523" data-source-node="n57"></a>Master BOL Type Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates what kind of master BOL you want the system to 
 create:</p>
        <ul type="disc" class="ul_1" data-source-node="n59">
            <li data-source-node="n60"><i data-source-node="n61">Consolidator</i>: 
 This type of master BOL is used when you have a number of shipments going 
 to different final destinations, but are all transported to a consolidation 
 point (a "freight consolidator") first. The master BOL would 
 then display the total shipments destined for this consolidator, and each 
 shipment's BOL will show the shipment's final destination. Note that the consolidator address 
 will display as the "Ship To Address" on the master BOL document.<br data-source-node="n62"> </li>
            <li data-source-node="n63"><i data-source-node="n64">Multi Stop TL</i>: 
 This type of master BOL is used for a multi stop truck load (TL). In this 
 case, the master BOL document displays the shipments for the entire trip, 
 and each shipment’s BOL represents one of the stops along the delivery 
 route. NOTE: For the "Ship To 
 Address" on the master BOL, the system will display the address on 
 the shipment that has the highest stop sequence number.<br data-source-node="n65"> </li>
            <li data-source-node="n66"><i data-source-node="n67">None</i>: 
 Choose this option if you do not want to generate a master BOL document.</li>
        </ul>
