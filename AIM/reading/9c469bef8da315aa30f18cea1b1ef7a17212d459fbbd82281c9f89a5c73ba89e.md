
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor530" data-source-node="n57"></a>Destination Yard Location Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the yard location where a trailer will be moved to at a later time. For example, if the yard location identified in the Assign To Location Field is a general drop off area, the yard location in this field would be the actual yard location where this trailer will eventually be placed. This is an optional field for Yard Check In.</p>
