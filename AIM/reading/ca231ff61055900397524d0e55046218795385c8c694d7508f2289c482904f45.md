
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor985" data-source-node="n57"></a>Status Action Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies which status will be 
 assigned to a record that does not follow a conventional status flow. 
 Status actions are usually used to accommodate canceled records, non-interfaced 
 records and custom modifications to the system.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">For a list of the default status actions, 
 see <a href="dc61935b07187c72f6d0d3ffab451fc810e7680007c33f4f1739ac5348bc27e5.html" data-source-node="n61">Reviewing or Creating Status 
 Actions</a>.</p>
