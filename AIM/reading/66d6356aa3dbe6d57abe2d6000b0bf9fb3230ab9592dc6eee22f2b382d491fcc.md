
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1797" data-source-node="n57"></a>Base Quantity Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the original quantity 
 of single units that were in a particular receipt container pre-check in. You 
 can use it to verify that the receipt container you are receiving contains the 
 amount of product expected by the system for that unit of measure.</p>
