Carrier Group

A carrier group limits the selection of carriers that the system evaluates for rating. When a carrier group is assigned to a shipment, the system will consider only the carriers within that group and choose the most suitable one. This approach enhances processing efficiency and allows for better control over carrier selection. Before carriers can be added to a carrier group, they must first be defined in the Carrier Window. Additionally, carrier groups can be integrated with dock management to refine the pool of containers eligible for dock location assignment during the dock assignment process.

 

Create Carrier Group Record

  • Enter Carrier Group and Description.

Define Group Type

Use the Group Type field to specify the purpose of the carrier group:

  • Rating: When a shipment is assigned a carrier group of this type, the system will consider only the carriers within that group for rating purposes and will select the most cost-effective carrier.

  • Dock Management: For dock assignments, the Dock Location Selection Strategy uses carrier groups to determine eligible dock areas for shipping containers. These carrier groups are linked to dock areas through the Dock Area Carrier Assignment window.

 

Rating Selection Option

  • Enable the Use Rating in Selection toggle button if you want the system to rate carriers within the group.

    When selected, and if at least two carriers can meet the shipment’s required date, the system will evaluate those carriers and select the lowest-cost option.

The Shortest Time Factor Field is reserved for future use.

 

Add Group Details

  • Select the carrier and service that you want to include in the group.

  • Use the Preference Factor field to assign a preference rating to each carrier. This acts as a tiebreaker when two or more carriers qualify based on the ship date.

    • The carrier with the lowest preference factor will be selected.

    • Example: If FedEx is given a preference factor of 1 and UPS a factor of 2, the system will choose FedEx if both meet the required ship date.
