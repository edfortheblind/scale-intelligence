
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1010" data-source-node="n57"></a>Ship To ID Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This value identifies the shipping address 
 associated with a specific customer. A customer can have multiple Ship To addresses. 
 This most commonly occurs if the customer is a retailer with multiple 
 stores that you support.</p>
