
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor888" data-source-node="n57"></a>Dock Area Button</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">Use this button to indicate that you want this dock location to be defined as an inventory-tracked dock area. Dock areas can either represent the "to location" during dock location assignment, or a collection of inventory-tracked dock positions. A dock area may also be used as an overflow area when all dock positions in another dock area have been filled to capacity.</p>
