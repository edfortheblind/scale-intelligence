
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1683" data-source-node="n57"></a>This Location Only Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">For a Company Transfer action, this field identifies that you want to 
 transfer the company for a single location's quantity only.  Note that company transfers can only be performed on locations where their 
 entire quantity is in an Available or  On 
 Hand status. You cannot perform a company transfer on a location where 
 any quantity is in an Allocated, In Transit and/or Suspended status. Also, 
 you cannot perform a company transfer on product at the shipping dock, 
 the receiving dock, or the receiving "pre-check in" location. Also the company transfer will happen only if the item master have multiple item/company records.</p>
