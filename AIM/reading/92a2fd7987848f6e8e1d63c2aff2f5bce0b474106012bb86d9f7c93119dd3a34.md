
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1325" data-source-node="n57"></a>Parent License Plate Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field represents the upper most level license plate in a location. For example, if you have two license plates that are nested together, then the "nested into" license plate is considered the parent, and the "nested" license plate is considered the child.</p>
