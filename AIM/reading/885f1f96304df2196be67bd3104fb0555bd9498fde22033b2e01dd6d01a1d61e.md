
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor403" data-source-node="n57"></a>Commercial Invoice</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">A commercial invoice is required for shipments from the United States 
 to Canada. It identifies how many containers are on a shipment, their 
 contents, the country of origin for the contents, etc. You will need to 
 print a commercial invoice when the container is manifested. Currently, 
 the commercial invoice accepts only one container per shipment. Commercial 
 invoices are generated for UPS shipments only. Commercial invoices are generated by Progistics, and routed to printers 
 in the system based on the values defined on the Document Routing Window.</p>
