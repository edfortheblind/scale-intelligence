        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">This value represents the charge returned 
 from Progistics with no accessorials or discounts applied. However, for 
 non-LTL carriers, this value represents the negotiated rate (if different 
 from standard), as Progistics does not track base rates for parcel carriers.</p>
