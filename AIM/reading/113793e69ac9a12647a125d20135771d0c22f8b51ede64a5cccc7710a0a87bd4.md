
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor317" data-source-node="n57"></a>Mark For ID Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The Mark For ID associated with this customer's 
 mark for address. The mark for address represents the final destination 
 for a shipment with a ship to address. A mark for address is typically required by 
 customers with internal distribution centers that service multiple stores. 
 The ship to address is the distribution center, where your shipments are 
 delivered. The customer will use the mark for address to determine where 
 it should ship the order (from the distribution center).</p>
