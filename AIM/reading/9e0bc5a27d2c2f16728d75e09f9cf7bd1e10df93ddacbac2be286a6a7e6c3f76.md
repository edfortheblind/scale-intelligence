
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1833" data-source-node="n57"></a>Export Port Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the port where the ship or plan docked when it 
 was exported with the export cargo. This value applies to international 
 shipments and appears on the Shipper's Export Declaration Form. </p>
