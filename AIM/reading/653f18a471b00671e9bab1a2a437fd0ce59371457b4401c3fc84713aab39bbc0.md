
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor996" data-source-node="n57"></a>Email Address Field</h1>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the email address associated 
 with a particular entity (such as a user, vendor, etc).</p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n61">On 
 the Warehouse Alerts Window...</h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n62">You can use this field to identify where you 
 will send a warehouse alert email message:</p>
        <ul type="disc" class="ul_1" data-source-node="n63">
            <li class="li_1" data-source-node="n64">Select 
 the Email Address Field to define the address that the system will 
 send the alert to (for example, customer@store.com).<br data-source-node="n65"> </li>
            <li class="li_1" data-source-node="n66">Select 
 the Ship From Email Address Button to send the alert to the email 
 address defined on the Ship From Tab of the Receipt Screen.<br data-source-node="n67">	 </li>
            <li class="li_1" data-source-node="n68">Select 
 the Vendor Email Address Button to send the alert to the email 
 address defined on the Source Tab of the Receipt Screen.<br data-source-node="n69"> </li>
            <li class="li_1" data-source-node="n70">Select 
 the Alternate Email Address Button to send the alert to the email 
 address defined on the General Tab of the Shipment Screen.</li>
        </ul>
