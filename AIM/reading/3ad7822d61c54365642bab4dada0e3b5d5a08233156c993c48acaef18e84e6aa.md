
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1397" data-source-node="n57"></a>Carrier Assignment Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates that you want to filter eligible containers by carrier/carrier service, carrier group, or none. </p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n60">Notes On "None" Records...</h5>
        <ul data-source-node="n61">
            <li data-source-node="n62">It is helpful to create a "none" record for a dock location area so that if the system cannot find an empty spot in the carrier's dock location area, then the container will be assigned to the overflow location (represented by the "none" record). <br data-source-node="n63"><br data-source-node="n64"></li>
            <li data-source-node="n65">If no "none" records are defined, then the system will assign the last dock location evaluated (regardless of its current open capacity).<br data-source-node="n66"></li>
        </ul>
