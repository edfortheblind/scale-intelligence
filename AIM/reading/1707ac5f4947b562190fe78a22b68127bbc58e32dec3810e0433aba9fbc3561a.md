
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1161" data-source-node="n57"></a>Quantity To Check In Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field is displayed on the Receipt Workbench and the Partial Quantity Check In screen. It identifies the amount 
 of received product that you will check in. The default value is the amount 
 expected, minus the amount already checked in. However, you can change 
 this value on the Partial Quantity Check In screen, if you want to check in 
 less than the expected amount. </p>
