
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1567" data-source-node="n57"></a>Location Criteria Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This record determines which location(s) the system will review for 
 possible replenishment. It also determines the order that the system will 
 review locations in a group. This record must be associated with a replenishment 
 master record.</p>
