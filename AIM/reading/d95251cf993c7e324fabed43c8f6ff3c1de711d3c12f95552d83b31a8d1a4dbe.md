
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor548" data-source-node="n57"></a>Creating/Managing Purchase Orders in Trading Partner 
 Management</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">Purchase orders indicate what item(s) your warehouse should be expecting 
 to arrive at the receiving dock. PO's are used by a warehouse's suppliers/vendors 
 to supply receipt information ahead of the arrival of the truck to their 
 warehouse. You can create and manage purchase orders via the web in the 
 Trading Partner Management module. Once a purchase order is created, you 
 can generate shipments from lines in the PO itself. This is done via the 
 web's Shipment Entry screen, which can be accessed from the Purchase Order 
 Management screen or the Trading Partner Management Main Menu. Once these 
 "shipments" are passed into SCALE, they become "receipts." 
 </p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">For example, suppose your warehouse's supplier is planning on sending 
 you 500 pallets of Item A over the course of a year. They would first 
 create a purchase order for Item A, adding a quantity of 500. When they 
 are ready to ship some of Item A to your warehouse, they would create 
 a shipment associated with this PO, which would indicate how many of the 
 item they are going to send out. When the year is over, and all the Item 
 A quantity has been shipped, the supplier would close the purchase order.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n61"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n62">From the Purchase Order Management screen, you can also print two documents: 
  one that 
 will display the PO information, and one that will display 
 a shipment's (receipt) information. Both of these documents 
 will spawn a separate web page window, allowing the user to print from 
 their browser.  </p>
        <p data-source-node="n63"> </p>
        <p class="p_4" data-source-node="n64">NOTE: Remember 
 that "shipments" in TPM become "receipts" when they 
 enter SCALE.</p>

        <h4 data-source-node="n81">To Start...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n82">Log into Trading Partner Management. Choose 
 the Purchase Order Management option from the Trading Partner Management 
 Main Menu. If it is the first time that you have logged onto the Purchase 
 Order module, you will need to agree with the license agreement. When 
 you press OK, the system will display the primary purchase order management 
 screen.</p>

        <h4 data-source-node="n86">Primary Purchase Order Screen Actions...</h4>
        <p class="p_5" data-source-node="n87"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n88">  Searching For Purchase Orders</h5>
        <p data-source-node="n89">You can use the fields on the primary purchase order management screen 
 to search for specific purchase order records. When the system displays 
 your search results, you can perform actions on the purchase orders (these 
 are described in the procedures listed below).</p>
        <p class="p_5" data-source-node="n90"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n91">  Creating a Purchase Order</h5>
        <p data-source-node="n92">Press the Create PO Button on primary purchase order management screen 
 to create a new purchase order in TPM:</p>
        <p data-source-node="n93"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n94"> </p>

        <h4 data-source-node="n96">Purchase Order Results Screen Actions...</h4>
        <p class="p_5" data-source-node="n97"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n98">Adding Shipment(s) to a Purchase Order</h5>
        <p class="p_5" data-source-node="n99">You can add any number of shipments to a purchase order. You can do 
 this on the purchase order results screen, by choosing the "page" 
 icon on the right side of the results line. Note that if you can only 
 add items to a shipment if they are already associated with a purchase 
 order. For detailed procedures on adding shipments, see the topic "Entering 
 a Web Shipment." </p>
        <p class="p_5" data-source-node="n100"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n101">Editing Purchase Orders</h5>
        <p class="p_5" data-source-node="n102">You can edit the details of a purchase order 
 by choosing thefirst icon on the 
 right side of the results line.</p>
        <p class="p_5" data-source-node="n103"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n104"> Printing a Purchase Order Document </h5>
        <p class="p_5" data-source-node="n105">You can print a document that displays a purchase 
 order and its associated shipments.</p>
        <p class="p_5" data-source-node="n106"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n107"> Closing Purchase Orders </h5>
        <p class="p_5" data-source-node="n108">You can close a purchase order by choosing the 
 "checkmark" icon on the right side of the results line. When 
 you close a purchase order, the system will ask you to confirm that you 
 want to do this action. Keep in mind that if you close a PO, then you 
 cannot make any further edits or add any additional shipments.</p>
        <p class="p_5" data-source-node="n109"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n110"> Deleting Purchase Orders</h5>
        <p class="p_5" data-source-node="n111">You can delete a purchase order by choosing the 
 "X" icon on the right side of the results line. When you delete 
 a PO, the system not only deletes the PO record but any associated shipments 
 as well.</p>
