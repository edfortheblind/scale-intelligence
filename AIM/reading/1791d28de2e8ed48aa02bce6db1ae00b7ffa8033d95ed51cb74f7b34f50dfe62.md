
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1630" data-source-node="n57"></a>Shipment Volume Range Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the volume span that a routing guide will support. 
 During routing, the system will compare the shipment's volume to the values 
 defined here.</p>
        <ul data-source-node="n59">
            <li data-source-node="n60">
                <p data-source-node="n61">If the shipment's volume falls 
 between this range, the routing guide will be assigned to the shipment 
 provided all of the other criteria match or are within range, and there 
 is not another record with more matching criteria.</p>
                <p data-source-node="n62"> </p>
            </li>
            <li data-source-node="n63">If the shipment's 
 volume does not fall between this range, the routing guide will 
 not be assigned to this shipment.</li>
        </ul>
