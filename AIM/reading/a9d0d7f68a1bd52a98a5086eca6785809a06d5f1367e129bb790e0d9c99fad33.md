
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1785" data-source-node="n57"></a>Container Manifest</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This document is a basic summary of the container(s) included on a manifest, including such information as the number of packages and charges. You 
 will need to print a container manifest when a manifest is closed. A container manifest is used by the truck driver so the driver knows what parcels 
 are on the truck. Container manifests are generated in Progistics, and routed to printers through the application. You can define this document's 
 printer on the Document Routing Window. Note that the term "container manifest" is a SCALE term for this 
 document. Carriers may instead refer to these documents as "parcel 
 manifests" or "summary manifests."</p>
