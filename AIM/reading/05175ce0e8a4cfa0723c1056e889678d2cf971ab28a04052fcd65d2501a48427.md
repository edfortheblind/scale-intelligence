
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor664" data-source-node="n57"></a>Pallet UM Conversion Criteria Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field  determines the parent containers that are eligible for the pallet UM conversion process. This process involves taking a full parent container (such as a pallet) and generating containers to represent its nested child containers, primarily for the purpose of printing vendor labels.</p>
