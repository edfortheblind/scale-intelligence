        <p data-source-node="n58">A zone type indicates the general function of zone(s) in your warehouse. 
 You can review the system-created zone types on the Zone Type Window. 
 If your require additional zone types, you may create them on the Zone 
 Type Window. </p>

        <h4 data-source-node="n83">Zone Types...<br data-source-node="n84"></h4>
        <ul data-source-node="n85">
            <li data-source-node="n86">  Locating Zones group together 
 locations that share the same locating requirements.<br data-source-node="n87"><br data-source-node="n88"></li>
            <li data-source-node="n89">Allocation Zones group 
 together locations that share the same allocating requirements.<br data-source-node="n90"><br data-source-node="n91"></li>
            <li data-source-node="n92">Work Zones group together 
 locations serviced by the same work teams and equipment types. In addition, 
 the same work types are performed in a work zone.</li>
        </ul>
