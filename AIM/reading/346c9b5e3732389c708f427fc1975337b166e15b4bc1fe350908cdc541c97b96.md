
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor998" data-source-node="n57"></a>Company Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">A unique entity that ships product from your 
 warehouse, usually under its own brand name. The system can support multiple companies 
 in one warehouse. Each company can have its own customer base and item(s) 
 in the system. This also allows you to set up unique item/company and 
 customer/company relationships in the same warehouse. For example, you 
 could have an item associated with two different companies, and the system 
 would be able to track this item independently for each company.</p>
