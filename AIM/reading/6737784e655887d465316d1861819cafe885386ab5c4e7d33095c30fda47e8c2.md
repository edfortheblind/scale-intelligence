
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1133" data-source-node="n57"></a>Shipping Container List</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">A shipping container list displays all of the containers associated 
 with a particular shipping load. The purpose of this generic document 
 is to provide the option to print this type of list regardless of what 
 carrier you are using. Also, you can easily change this document's field 
 layout/design to conform to any carrier's specification.  </p>
