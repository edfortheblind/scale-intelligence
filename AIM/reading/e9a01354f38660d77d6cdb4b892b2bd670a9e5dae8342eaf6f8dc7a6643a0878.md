
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor88" data-source-node="n57"></a>Labor Group Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">A labor group represents a collection of warehouse users that perform related work duties, such as picking product for a certain company's shipments or work details. The labor group records in the application define such employee information as how many people (full time and temporary) who work at your warehouse, as well as what quantity units of measure they typically process. The labor groups are linked to work types on the Work Type configuration.</p>
