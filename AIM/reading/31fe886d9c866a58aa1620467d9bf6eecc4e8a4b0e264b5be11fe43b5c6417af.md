
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor72" data-source-node="n57"></a>Loading Pier Field</h1>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the name/number of the 
 pier or terminal where cargo is loaded for exportation. This value applies 
 to international shipments and appears on the Shipper's Export Declaration 
 Form. </p>
