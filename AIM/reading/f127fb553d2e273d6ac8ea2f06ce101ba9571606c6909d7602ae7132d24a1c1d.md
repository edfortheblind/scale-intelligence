
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1877" data-source-node="n57"></a>From Work Zone Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the from work zone associated 
 with a work instruction. It appears on the Work Instruction Window. The from work zone identifies where product 
 will be obtained to fulfill an order. You can create a work special handling record 
 that requires employees to identify the work zone for specific transactions.</p>
