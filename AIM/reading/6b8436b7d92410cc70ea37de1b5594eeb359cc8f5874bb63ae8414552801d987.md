
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n57"><a name="kanchor310" data-source-node="n58"></a>Zone Type Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n59">A zone type identifies the purpose of a zone. These are predefined in 
 the system. A zone can be associated with multiple zones.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n61">The system supports the following zone types:</p>
        <p data-source-node="n62"> </p>
        <table style="border-spacing: 1px 1px;margin-left: 0;margin-right: auto" cellspacing="0" data-source-node="n63">
            <col style="width: 92px" data-source-node="n64">
            <col style="width: 941px" data-source-node="n65">
            <tr data-source-node="n66">
                <td valign="top" class="td_3" data-source-node="n67">
                    <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n68"><i data-source-node="n69">Allocating</i>
                    </p>
                </td>
                <td valign="top" class="td_3" data-source-node="n70">
                    <p data-source-node="n71">Use this zone type to establish the different location groups that you 
 want to move product from. When the system runs the allocation plan, it 
 will search the Picking Rule Sets to determine which allocation zones 
 to process.</p>
                    <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n72"> </p>
                </td>
            </tr>
            <tr data-source-node="n73">
                <td valign="top" class="td_3" data-source-node="n74">
                    <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n75"><i data-source-node="n76">Locating</i>
                    </p>
                </td>
                <td valign="top" class="td_3" data-source-node="n77">
                    <p data-source-node="n78">Use this zone type to establish the different location groups that you 
 want the system to move product to. For example, many warehouse establish 
 one zone for locating loose picks and another for locating bulk picks.</p>
                    <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n79"> </p>
                </td>
            </tr>
            <tr data-source-node="n80">
                <td valign="top" class="td_3" data-source-node="n81">
                    <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n82"><i data-source-node="n83">Work</i>
                    </p>
                </td>
                <td valign="top" class="td_3" data-source-node="n84">
                    <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n85">Use this zone type to assign teams to particular 
 locations. Configure the warehouse so each section requiring different 
 equipment or picking constraints is a unique zone. Since teams work specific 
 zones, this ensures that the appropriate resources are used in each zone.</p>
                    <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n86"> </p>
                </td>
            </tr>
        </table>
