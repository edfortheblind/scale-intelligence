
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1971" data-source-node="n57"></a>Quantity Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The item amount associated with a work order, a purchase order,
 a receipt line, a receipt container, a shipment line, a work 
 order, a shipping container, or an inventory adjustment.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">When this field is specified in the Receipt From Purchase Order Screen, you can mention a value here and click "Update all lines" to assign the receipt quantity on all of the lines as the quantity specified.</p>
