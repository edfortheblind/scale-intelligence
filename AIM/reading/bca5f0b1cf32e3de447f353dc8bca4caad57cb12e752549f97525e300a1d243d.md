
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor633" data-source-node="n57"></a>Putwall Location Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">Putwall Location is the unique place in the warehouse  used for sorting inventory batch picked in the totes. </p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">These putwall locations are created with <b data-source-node="n61">Location class - Putwall</b></p>
        <p data-source-node="n62"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n63">These locations will represent the putwall sort locations in the warehouse. However these locations will not be inventory tracked or displayed on search in the Inventory insight screen. Users can view the putwall location in the <a href="2755b39d16d7150ac381808895d023b90f2e186ba8398a9aa4cbbfef1f8bfa53.html" data-source-node="n64">Putwall Insight</a> screen.</p>
