
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor102" data-source-node="n57"></a>Name Field</h1>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the name of the vendor, 
 person, company, or customer that you want to identify in the system. 
 Generally, a value is provided in a name field for contact purposes. This 
 field is included on numerous setup windows, including the Warehouse Window, 
 Vendor Window, and Company Window. Valid values are dependent on the type 
 of record that you are creating.</p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n61">On the Shipment Window...</h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n62">This field identifies the name of the person 
 in your warehouse who can sign for an international shipment. This person 
 is responsible for verifying the truth and accuracy about an international 
 shipment's contents.</p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n63"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n64"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n65">On the VAS Activity Window...</h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n66">This field identifies a VAS activity record. This is the name that the system will label an activity in the VAS Container: Insight Screen and other windows.</p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n67"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n68"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n69">On the QC Assignment Window...</h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n70">This field identifies the name of a QC Assignment record.</p>
