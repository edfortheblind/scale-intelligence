
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1302" data-source-node="n57"></a>From Location Field</h1>
        <h5 data-source-node="n58">In Labor Management...</h5>
        <p data-source-node="n59">This field identifies the location from which 
 you are transferring inventory using the Labor Activity Insight Screen. The inventory is picked from the 'From Location'. </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n60"> </h5>
        <h5 data-source-node="n61">In Inventory Management... </h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n62">This field identifies the location from which 
 you are transferring inventory using the Inventory Transfer screen.</p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n63"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n64"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n65">In Work Execution...</h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n66">This field identifies where product will be 
 picked during work execution.</p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n67"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n68"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n69">In Replenishment...</h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n70">This field identifies the location where product will be taken from (typically a bulk storage location) when replenishing locations with a diminished quantity.</p>
