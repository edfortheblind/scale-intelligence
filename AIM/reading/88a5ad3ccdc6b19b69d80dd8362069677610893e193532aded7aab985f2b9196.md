
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor235" data-source-node="n57"></a>Parent Instruction Number Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the parent instruction 
 number associated with a work instruction. The parent record can either 
 be a header or a summary record. It is an internal identifier and is not 
 displayed on the either the RF device or the pick list. Parent instruction numbers are generated during 
 work creation.</p>
