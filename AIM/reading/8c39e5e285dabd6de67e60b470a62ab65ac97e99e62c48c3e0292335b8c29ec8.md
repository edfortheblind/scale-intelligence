
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1216" data-source-node="n57"></a>Defining a Freight Consolidator Record</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">A freight consolidator represents the place where a shipping load will 
 delivered to, in lieu of its final destination. This configuration stores 
 the address and contact information for each consolidator. Freight consolidators 
 are used when generating a "consolidator" type master bill of 
 lading. This address will display as the "Ship To Address" on 
 the master BOL document. You can assign a consolidator to a load in the 
 Shipping Load screen.</p>
