
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56">Note On Serial Number Archiving</h1>
        <h4 data-source-node="n57">Inbound Serial Number Archiving...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n58">If an item is defined for inbound 
 serial number tracking (without inventory tracking), the system 
 will archive the serial number when the receipt is closed. This keeps 
 only the active serial numbers in the active serial number table. After 
 archival, these serial numbers will still show up on the Receipt Container 
 screen, any receipt container paperwork, and various search screens. 
 </p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <h4 data-source-node="n61">Outbound Serial Number Archiving...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n62">If an item is defined for outbound 
 serial number tracking, the system will archive the serial numbers 
 once the shipment has been ship confirmed. This keeps only the active 
 serial numbers in the active serial number table, and allows for serial 
 numbers to re-enter the warehouse as a return. After archival, the serial 
 numbers will still show up on the Shipping Container screen, any shipping 
 container paperwork, and various search screens.</p>
