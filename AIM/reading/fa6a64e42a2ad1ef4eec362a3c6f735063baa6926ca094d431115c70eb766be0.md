
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor950" data-source-node="n57"></a>To Location Field</h1>
        <h5 data-source-node="n58">In Labor Management...</h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n59">This field identifies the location to which 
 you are transferring inventory using the Labor Activity Insight screen. The inventory is putaway at 'To Location'. </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n61"> </h5>
        <h5 data-source-node="n62">In Inventory Management... </h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n63">This field identifies the location to which 
 you are transferring inventory using the Inventory Transfer screen.</p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n64"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n65"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n66">In Work Execution...</h5>
        <p class="p_19" data-source-node="n67">This field identifies where product will be 
 putaway during work execution.</p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n68">  </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n69"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n70">In Replenishment... </h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n71">This field identifies the location (typically a forward picking location) that is eligible for replenishment because of diminished quantity.</p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n72"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n73"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n74">In Immediate Needs Insight...</h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n75">This field indicates the destination location of the immediate need.</p>
