
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1044" data-source-node="n57"></a>Priority Escalate By Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the number of digits that you want the system to update an instruction/unit up in priority (from its original priority) each time the work priority escalation scheduled job is run. Note that you can enter a negative number into this field, and the priority would be escalated higher (say, from 5 to 10).</p>
