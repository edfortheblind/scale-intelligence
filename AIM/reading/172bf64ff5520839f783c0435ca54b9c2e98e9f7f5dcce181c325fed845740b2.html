Resolving Inbound QC Results After Inspection (RF & Non-RF)

Once an item quantity's inbound quality control inspection has been completed, you will need to manually resolve this inventory out of the inbound QC location. Exactly what this entails will depend on whether the quantity passes or fails the inspection.

 

 

If the item quantity PASSES inspection...

If the quantity passes inspection you will need to perform two actions in order to resolve the quantity. First, you will need to change the status of the item quantity to an allocatable status. Second, you will need to transfer this quantity from the QC location to a normal inventory location. Note that you can also choose to leave this inventory in the QC location if you plan on allocating the quantity directly from there.

 

You can perform these actions via full-screen or RF inventory management (location transfers, status adjustments)

 

 

If the item quantity FAILS inspection...

If the quantity fails inspection you will need to perform several actions, depending on your particular business practices. First, you will need to make sure that the quantity stays in an unallocatable status. This is done so the system does not allocate any of this "bad" quantity for outbound shipments. You may also want to move this quantity to some sort of holding location. Finally, depending on your business practices, you may need to then destroy the quantity, send it back to the vendor, etc. This would also involve negatively adjusting the item quantity, so it is no longer available in the system.

 

You can perform these actions via full-screen or RF inventory management (item adjustments, location transfers, status adjustments)
