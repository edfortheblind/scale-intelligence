
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor890" data-source-node="n57"></a>Minimum Weight Field</h1>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the minimum weight that 
 a shipment's contents must equal for an email alert to be triggered. It 
 works in unison with the other fields on the Email Alerts Window: the 
 criteria defined in the window's other fields must also be met before 
 the alert can be triggered. </p>
