
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="InConfirmation" data-source-node="n57"></a><a name="kanchor1428" data-source-node="n58"></a>In Confirmation Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n59">This field indicates that the specific shipment or work order is literally in the middle of being 'confirmed'. This option ensures that multiple users cannot try to confirm a load or work order concurrently. </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n61"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n62">On the Shipping Load Insight Screen:</h5>
        <p data-source-node="n63">The In Confirmation icon (Yellow color) under List Pane Grid Field indicates that the shipping load is in the process of being confirmed.</p>
