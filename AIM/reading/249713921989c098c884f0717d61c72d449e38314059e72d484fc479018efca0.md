
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1977" data-source-node="n57"></a>Break Hours Per Day Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the number of hours that an employee is regularly 
 scheduled to take breaks (lunch, etc). Note that you can provide partial-hour 
 totals in this field by using decimal values (such as 1.5 hours).</p>
