
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1748" data-source-node="n57"></a>Carrier Group Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">A carrier group is a subset of carriers. They 
 are often used to group together carriers that offer similiar services. 
 If a carrier group is associated with a shipment, the system will select 
 the shipping carrier from that carrier group. Carrier groups allow you to minimize processing 
 time during rating and routing. They also provide you with greater control 
 during the rating process.</p>
