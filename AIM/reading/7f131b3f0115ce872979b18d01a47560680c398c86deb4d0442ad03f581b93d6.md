
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor54" data-source-node="n57"></a>Total Transactions Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the number of transactions associated with a labor 
 management record. For example, if an employee executed a picking work 
 unit w/ten instructions, then the value in the Total Transactions Field 
 for that work unit's labor record would be "10."</p>
