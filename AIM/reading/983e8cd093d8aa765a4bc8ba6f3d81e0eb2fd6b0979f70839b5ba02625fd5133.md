
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor180" data-source-node="n57"></a>Equipment Type / Authorized Equipment Type Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The type of equipment (such as a forklift) used to process work. You 
 can associate an equipment type with a team or a user. Note that if you 
 do not associate an equipment type with a specific user/team, it can be 
 used by all users/teams. </p>
