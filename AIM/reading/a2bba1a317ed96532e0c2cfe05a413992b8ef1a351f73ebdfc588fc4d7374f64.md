        <p data-source-node="n58">This field represents how much product the person processed in a minute. The system obtains the rate by performing the following calculation against the Labor Management Detail record:</p>
        <ul data-source-node="n59">
            <li data-source-node="n60">Total Quantity / Total Actual Time = Actual Rate</li>
        </ul>
