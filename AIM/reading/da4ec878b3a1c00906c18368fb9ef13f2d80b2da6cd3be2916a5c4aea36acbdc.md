
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor730" data-source-node="n57"></a>Transportation Mode Field</h1>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">The method of transport for the cargo being exported. This value applies 
 to international shipments and appears on the Shipper's Export Declaration 
 Form. This value must be the full name of the transportation 
 mode, not an abbreviation or acronym. Common values include vessel, rail, 
 air and truck. If you are transporting the product yourself, use the value 
 'own power'.</p>
