
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor356" data-source-node="n57"></a>Screen Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field allows you to tie the action tile to a called screen on the Warehouse dashboard.  The specified screen will be called when you click the action tile. If you leave this field blank, the action tile on the dashboard will not be clickable.  </p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">NOTE: Only screens that are available from the system menu are available to be called from action tiles.  </p>
