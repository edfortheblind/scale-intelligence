
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1809" data-source-node="n57"></a>Serial Number Options Checkboxes</h1>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">You can use the serial number option checkboxes 
 on the Item Window to determine how the system will track serial numbers 
 for an item. You will need to first choose which warehouse flow (Inbound, 
 Inventory, or Outbound) for which you want the system to track serial 
 numbers. </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n60">The application allows you to 
 track serial numbers in the following combinations:</p>
        <blockquote data-source-node="n61">
            <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n62">- Inbound only</p>
            <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n63">
                <br data-source-node="n64">- Inventory only</p>
            <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n65">
                <br data-source-node="n66">- Outbound only</p>
            <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n67">
                <br data-source-node="n68">- Inbound and Outbound</p>
            <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n69">
                <br data-source-node="n70">- Inbound and Inventory and Outbound</p>
        </blockquote>
