External Rating Symbol Field

On the Dimension UM Window...

This field identifies the value (which corresponds to the Dimension UM) that is sent by the system to the transportation engine, either FSMS or Progistics. Valid values for this field are "I" (for inches) and "C" (for centimeters).

 

 

On the Freight Terms Window...

This field indicates the Progistics name for a freight term. The system passes this symbol to Progistics when the system requests a rate.
