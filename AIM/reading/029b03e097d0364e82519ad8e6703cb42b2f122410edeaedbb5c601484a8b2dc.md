
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor2084" data-source-node="n57"></a>Freight Term Field</h1>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">The type of freight term associated with a 
 shipment. The freight term determines how a shipment's billing will be 
 managed. During rating, the freight term is passed to Progistics. 
 The system can also print this information on paperwork.</p>
