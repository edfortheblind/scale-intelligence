Creating Custom Status Flows

A custom status flow accommodates the processing needs of special or unique items. Custom flows are defined per functional area. They cannot be shared across functional areas: in other words, a custom flow associated with the outbound functional area cannot also be used by the inbound functional area.

Using the Header Fields
  1. Use the Flow Name Field to provide an identifier for your custom status flow. Also provide a description.
     
  2. Use the Functional Area Field to indicate if your flow will be Outbound (shipment processing) or Inbound (receipt processing).Z

 

Using the Detail Records Tab
  1. Use the Status Name Checkbox to include a status in the flow. You create the steps of the status flow by selecting its statuses from this tab. All mandatory statuses are checked. They are automatically included in this new flow and cannot be deselected. If you select one with a related status, the system will include both of these statuses in the flow.
