
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1973" data-source-node="n57"></a>Net Price Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the actual sales price 
 of an item. The system uses this value throughout the system to determine 
 the "value" of a shipment (which is subsequently rolled up to 
 the Total Value field), and to display on a Commercial Invoice for international 
 shipments.</p>
