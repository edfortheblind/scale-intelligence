
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1610" data-source-node="n57"></a>Email Template Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the email template that the system will use to 
 generate a specific warehouse alert type. All templates (.txt files) must 
 reside in the Printing Directory on your system server. The email template determines the structure 
 and contents of the alert message. You can associate an email template 
 with a general email alert type in the Email Alert Type Window. You may 
 also associate a template with a specific customer's alert message in 
 the Email Alerts Window.</p>
