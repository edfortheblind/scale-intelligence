
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor900" data-source-node="n57"></a>To Work Zone Field</h1>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the to work zone associated 
 with a work instruction. The to work zone identifies where product 
 will be delivered to fulfill a work instruction. The system will check 
 the authority of a user, team or equipment type for each work zone. </p>
