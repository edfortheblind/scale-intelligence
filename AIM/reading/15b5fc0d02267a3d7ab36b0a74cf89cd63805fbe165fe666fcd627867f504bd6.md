
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1294" data-source-node="n57"></a>Preference Factor Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the preference factor associated with a carrier</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59">within a carrier group. This field is numeric.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n61">When the Use rating in selection is checked in the carrier group, and if multiple carrier services can meet the ship date/delivery date requirements, the system first chooses the carrier service with the lowest freight rate. When there is a tie then the Preference Factor gets executed.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n62">Also when the Use rating in selection is unchecked, then the system considers Days to deliver over the preference factor. Only if there is a tie in days to deliver, the system will use the preference factor.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n63">For 
 example, if Fed Ex is assigned the preference factor 1 and UPS is assigned 
 the preference factor 2, the system will select Fed Ex instead of UPS, 
 assuming that both carriers can meet the required ship date.</p>
