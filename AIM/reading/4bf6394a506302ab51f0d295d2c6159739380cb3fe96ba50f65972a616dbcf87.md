        <h4 data-source-node="n70"><a name="Close" data-source-node="n71"></a>Close Putaway Group </h4>
        <p data-source-node="n72">The following details the steps for the </p>
        <ol data-source-node="n73">
            <li value="1" data-source-node="n74">Log in to Warehouse Mobile application.</li>
            <li value="2" data-source-node="n75">Select the <b data-source-node="n76">Close Putaway Group</b> option from the Warehouse Mobile Main Menu.</li>
            <li value="3" data-source-node="n77">Enter/Scan Putaway group id of the putaway group that you want to close and tap <b data-source-node="n78">Go</b>.</li>
            <li value="4" data-source-node="n79">Navigate to the action buttons and tap <b data-source-node="n80">Close</b>. A success message is displayed. Note that if your receiving preferences are configured to create work, work creation will occur at this time.</li>
        </ol>
        <h4 data-source-node="n81">Auto Close Putaway Group</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n82">Assigning license plates to a putaway group is automatically closes the putaway group when the total license plate quantity being assigned to the putaway group is reaching or exceeding the putaway group’s maximum units. The Maximum units field value set in the Putaway Location Group is considered putaway group’s maximum units.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n83"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n84">For example:</p>
        <ul data-source-node="n85">
            <li data-source-node="n86">
                <p data-source-node="n87">A putaway location group with a maximum capacity of 4 units will automatically close the putaway group when a license plate with 3 EA and another with 1 EA are assigned.</p>
            </li>
            <li data-source-node="n88">
                <p data-source-node="n89">checking in and locating two receipt containers (each containing 9 EA) creates two putaway location groups when the maximum units are set to 9. The system automatically closes both putaway group.</p>
            </li>
        </ul>
