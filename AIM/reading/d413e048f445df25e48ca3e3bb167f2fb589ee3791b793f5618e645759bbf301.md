
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1609" data-source-node="n57"></a>Locations Indicator Tile</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">Locations indicator tile on the Lot Insight screen's Detail Pane shows the number of locations in the warehouse for the selected item, company, and lot in the warehouse.  Clicking on this tile opens the Inventory Insight screen filtered by the item, company, and lot in the warehouse.</p>
