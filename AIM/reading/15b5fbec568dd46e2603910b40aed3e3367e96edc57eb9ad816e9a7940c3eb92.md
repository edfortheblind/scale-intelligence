
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor758" data-source-node="n57"></a>Making an Item Eligible For Immediate Needs</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">For the system to alert the user of the immediate needs of a rejected 
 entity (shipment, receipt, or work order), you must first activate the 
 item(s) that you want the system to track immediate needs for. Note that components and finished 
 items associated with the "Add BOM Component Lines" wave step 
 are ineligible for immediate needs processing. You can complete this step on the Handling (1) Tab of the Item Window.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n61">To Make an Item Eligible...</h5>
        <p data-source-node="n62">Access the Item Window. Open the item you wish to make eligible for 
 immediate needs.</p>
        <ol class="ol_1" data-source-node="n63">
            <li value="1" data-source-node="n64">Access the Handling 
 (1) Tab. <br data-source-node="n65"> </li>
            <li value="2" data-source-node="n66">Use the Eligible 
 Checkbox to indicate that you want this item to be made eligible 
 for immediate needs processing.<br data-source-node="n67"> </li>
            <li value="3" data-source-node="n68">Use the Locating 
 Rule Field to identify the rule you want the system to use when 
 locating quantity of this item to fulfill immediate needs.<br data-source-node="n69"> </li>
            <li value="4" data-source-node="n70">Press OK. 
 The system will save your changes.</li>
        </ol>
