
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1646" data-source-node="n57"></a>COD Amount Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The COD (Cash On Delivery) amount refers to 
 the payment for the goods being shipped. If there is a COD Amount applied 
 to a shipment, the carrier cannot deliver the shipment until payment has 
 been received.</p>
