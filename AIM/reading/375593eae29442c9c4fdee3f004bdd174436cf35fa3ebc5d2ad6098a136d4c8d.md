
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor487" data-source-node="n57"></a>Instruction Number Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the work instruction 
 number associated with a shipment line. It is an internal identifier. 
 This value is not displayed on the either the RF device or the pick list 
 unless it defined as the work unit on the Work configuration. Instruction numbers are generated during work 
 execution.</p>
