
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor699" data-source-node="n57"></a>Execute Group Putaway Checkbox</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This checkbox indicates that you want users (assigned to a specific 
 receiving preference) to be eligible to process group putaways when locating 
 product. When these users locate received items, each item will be assigned 
 to one "putaway group," according to the group's settings (as 
 defined on the Putaway Group Window).</p>
