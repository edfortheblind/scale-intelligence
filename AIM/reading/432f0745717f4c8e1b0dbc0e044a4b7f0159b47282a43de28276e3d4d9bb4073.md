        <p data-source-node="n58">The unit of measure that your company uses 
 to determine weight. You can use any unit of measure. However, 
 this value must apply to all items, containers, receipts, receipt 
 lines, shipments, shipment lines, and shipping loads defined in 
 the warehouse.</p>
