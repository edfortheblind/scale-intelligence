            <li data-source-node="n63"><i data-source-node="n64">For Inbound receipts</i>, this field is referred to as the License Plate (LP) ID.<br data-source-node="n65"><br data-source-node="n66"></li>
            <li data-source-node="n67"><i data-source-node="n68">For Inventory storage</i>, this field is referred to as the License Plate (LP) ID.</li>
