
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1444" data-source-node="n57"></a>Allow Over Receiving Checkbox</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">You can use this checkbox if the employee can receive more product than 
 is identified in the Original Total Quantity Field on the receipt line. 
 If the employee does over receive, the 
 system will not display any warning message or require any overrides on 
 an RF device. Over receiving is often used by organizations that receive product 
 from their own manufacturing department.</p>
