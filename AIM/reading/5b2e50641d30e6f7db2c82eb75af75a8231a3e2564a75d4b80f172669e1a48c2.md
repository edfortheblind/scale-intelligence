
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1303" data-source-node="n57"></a>Location Range End Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the last location in location range. A location 
 range is a series of locations where product can be picked for a picking 
 group. Locations are considered within a range if they fall between the start 
 and end locations alphabetically and/or numerically.</p>
