
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1472" data-source-node="n57"></a>CAGE Code Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies that an item is associated with a company that 
 does business with the United States Federal Government. The format of 
 this is a five-position code, where the first and fifth position must 
 be a numeric character. The second, third and fourth positions may be 
 any mixture of alpha/numeric characters (excluding "I" and "O"). 
 All positions are non-significant. Note that if you need to obtain a CAGE 
 Code, contact the Defense Logistics Information Service (DLIS).</p>
