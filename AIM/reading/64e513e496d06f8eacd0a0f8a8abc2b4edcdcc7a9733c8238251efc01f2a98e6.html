Performing an EDI Transmission

An EDI (Electronic Data Interchange) transmission sends manifest data electronically to the carrier. The transmission includes any piece of information that is associated with the manifest. Whether or not you are required to perform a transmission depends on the carrier associated with the manifest. When you close the manifest of a FedEx or UPS shipment, the system sends the EDI transmission automatically. The United States Postal Service (USPS) does not require an EDI transmission at this time. You send an EDI transmission when you close a manifest. To transmit during the closing process, see Processing a Manifest.

 

 

 

Prerequisites...

  • A manifest must be closed before you can perform an EDI Transmission. If the Transmit Status of the manifest is not available (N/A), then that carrier has not been set up to transmit EDI data.
