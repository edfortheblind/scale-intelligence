
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor536" data-source-node="n57"></a>Tracking Number Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The unique identifier assigned to a parcel when is manifested. Carriers 
 either assign tracking numbers or dictate its format.</p>
        <ul data-source-node="n59">
            <li data-source-node="n60">
                <p data-source-node="n61">If the parcel is manifested to 
 UPS, UPS dictates the tracking number format.</p>
                <p data-source-node="n62"> </p>
            </li>
            <li data-source-node="n63">
                <p data-source-node="n64">If the parcel is manifested to 
 USPS Priority with Delivery Confirmation, the value in this field 
 will represent the delivery confirmation number.</p>
                <p data-source-node="n65"> </p>
            </li>
            <li data-source-node="n66">
                <p data-source-node="n67">If the parcel is manifested to 
 FedEx, FedEx assigns the tracking number through SCALE.</p>
                <p data-source-node="n68"> </p>
            </li>
            <li data-source-node="n69">If the parcel 
 is manifested with another parcel carrier, Progistics assigns the 
 tracking number.</li>
        </ul>
