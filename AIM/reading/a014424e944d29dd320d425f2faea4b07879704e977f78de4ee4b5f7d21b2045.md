
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1730" data-source-node="n57"></a>Pad From Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">You will use this field to indicate if you want the system to add the 
 "pad character" on the left or right side of a fixed length 
 interface value. For example, suppose a field is configured to be 10 characters 
 in length, and your pad character is an asterisk. The actual value, however, 
 has only four characters (3212). The system, then, would output this value 
 as 3212***** or   ******3212, 
 depending on if you choose to pad from the left or the right.</p>
