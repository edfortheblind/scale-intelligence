
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1718" data-source-node="n57"></a>LTL Class Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The LTL class associated with an NMFC code. During rating, the system 
 passes the LTL class and item name to rating, which then calculates 
 the shipping rate. The system also prints the class on the shipment's 
 bill of lading. LTL classes are defined by the National Motor 
 Freight Carriers. They are often used by LTL and TL carriers to help calculate 
 shipping load rates. LTL carriers typically rate at the shipment level using the weight of the shipment by LTL class. Using the SCALE internal LTL rating, the system will look at the LTL class to determine the associated weight break and rate for the shipment.</p>
