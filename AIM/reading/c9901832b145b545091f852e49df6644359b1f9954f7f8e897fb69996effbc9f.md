
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1096" data-source-node="n57"></a>Pallet Building Master Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field defines how the system will build pallets during a wave that includes the pallet building wave step. This master record determines such things as which shipping container(s) will be eligible to be placed on a new pallet, and which strategy the system will use to arrange the pallet's contents.
</p>
