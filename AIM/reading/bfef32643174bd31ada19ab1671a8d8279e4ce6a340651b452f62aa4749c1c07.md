
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor20" data-source-node="n57"></a>Percent Complete Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The Percent Complete field indicates the percentage of cycle count requests on the plan that are no longer active. This percentage is calculated based on total closed divided by total open plus total closed where total open reflects both Open and Pending Review requests.</p>
