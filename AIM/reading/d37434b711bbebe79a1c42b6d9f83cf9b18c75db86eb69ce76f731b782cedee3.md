
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1617" data-source-node="n57"></a>Rating Service Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The name used to identify a carrier service to the rating system. The rating service is linked to the carrier in 
 the system on the Carrier Window. If you need additional rating services for 
 other carriers, contact your system representative. Although you can add 
 rating services yourself, we strongly 
 recommend that your system consultant define all rating services. When using Progistics, this value 
 is obtained from Progistics and defined on the following the system window: 
 Rating Service Window. If 
 a rating service is improperly defined, Progistics will be unable to generate 
 rates for any carrier associated with that service.</p>
