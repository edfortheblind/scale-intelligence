
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1488" data-source-node="n57"></a>Close Date/Time Field</h1>
        <h5 data-source-node="n58">On the Receipt Screen...</h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n59"> 
 This field identifies when all the quantity on a receipt is located (when 
 no work is created), or when all the quantity on a receipt is put away 
 (when work is created for locating).    Note that a receipt can be manually closed by the user regardless of what status 
 the receipt quantity is in. </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> </p>
