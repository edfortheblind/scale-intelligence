
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1963" data-source-node="n57"></a>Goal Quantity Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field displays how much the system expected the person to process. The system determines this value by performing the following calculation against the Labor Management Detail record:</p>
        <blockquote data-source-node="n59">
            <p data-source-node="n60">Total Quantity / Estimated Time Per Transaction = Goal Quantity</p>
        </blockquote>
