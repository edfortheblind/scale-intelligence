
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1410" data-source-node="n57"></a>Trading Partner Management Process Summary</h1>

        <h4 data-source-node="n62">Summary...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n63">Trading Partner Management is a website that acts as a supply chain 
 collaboration tool between the customer and the application. It allows 
 for real-time access of shipment status information over the Internet. 
 You can enter orders for items over the web, as well as see the status 
 of these orders. Also, this website can be configured to reflect the image 
 of any company located in your warehouse. This allows you to preserve 
 the multi-company environment of your warehouse. The TPM site includes five main functional areas: 
 Supplier Enablement (purchase order, shipment entry), Order Management (order entry, status), Statistics (inbound, item, outbound), and Inventory Management (inventory status). You can access these from the TPM Main Menu. </p>

        <h4 data-source-node="n76">Web Users...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n77">A web user is a person who logs onto Trading Partner Management. There 
 are two types of users: external or internal. External users can only 
 access their own shipment/receipt information. Examples of external users 
 are people not employed by your organization, including a customer, business 
 partner, or supplier. External users can access either a single company 
 or a select group of companies. Internal users, however, can access any 
 shipment/receipt information in the system. Examples of internal users 
 are employees working offsite or using the company intranet.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n78"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n79"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n80"> </p>
        <h4 data-source-node="n81">Single Company vs. Multi Company Processing...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n82">Trading Partner Management can be configured to reflect the image of 
 any company located in your warehouse. If your warehouse supports multiple 
 companies, you can define the appearance of your site to reflect each 
 company supported in your warehouse. If you are a single-company warehouse, 
 you can define the appearance of the website to reflect this. The system 
 can display your warehouse's name on the login page, and a warehouse graphic 
 on the main menu.</p>

        <h4 data-source-node="n119">Purchase Orders...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n120">Purchase orders indicate what item(s) your warehouse should be expecting 
 to arrive at the receiving dock. PO's are used by a warehouse's suppliers/vendors 
 to supply receipt information ahead of the arrival of the truck to their 
 warehouse. You can create and manage purchase orders on the TPM website. For example, suppose your warehouse's supplier is planning on sending 
 you 500 pallets of Item A over the course of a year. They would first 
 create a purchase order for Item A, adding a quantity of 500. When they 
 are ready to ship some of Item A to your warehouse, they would create 
 a shipment associated with this PO, which would indicate how many of the 
 item they are going to send out. When the year is over, and all the Item 
 A quantity has been shipped, the supplier would close the purchase order.</p>

        <h4 data-source-node="n124">Order Management...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n125">The order status component can be used to research the status 
 of orders entered via the Order Entry module. This is helpful if you want 
 to view the current status of your order in the warehouse. For information on the web order entry component, see the <a href="c8560c338e6a97414b70d7033501624f7b8184c248942b1d397328383f163a04.html" data-source-node="n126">Order Entry Process Summary</a> topic.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n127"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n128"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n129"> </p>
        <h4 data-source-node="n130">Web Statistics...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n131">The web statistics component can be used to research your warehouse's 
 receipts, inventory, and shipments. Users will enter search criteria information, 
 and the system will return summary data statistics that match the criteria. 
 The system can output these statistics in several formats, such as HTML 
 and XML.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n132"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n133"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n134"> </p>
        <h4 data-source-node="n135">Inventory Status...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n136">The inventory status component can be used to research the inventory 
 status of items in a warehouse. Users will enter criteria to search for 
 an item. The system will then display the appropriate item(s), and whether 
 they are available in the warehouse's inventory. This is helpful if you 
 want to check if an item is in stock before placing an order.</p>

        <h4 data-source-node="n140">Email Alerts...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n141">An email alert is a message the system sends to a customer when a warehouse 
 event occurs. The purpose of the message is to inform a customer as to 
 the status of their shipment. The system triggers alerts if a shipment 
 meets a certain system status, or if the system generates a certain piece 
 of documentation. Alerts are defined in the Email Alerts Window. 
 In this window, you can also indicate what email address you want the 
 alert to be sent, and the template you want the system to use to generate 
 the email.</p>
