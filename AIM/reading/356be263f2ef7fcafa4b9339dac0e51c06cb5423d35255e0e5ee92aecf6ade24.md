
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1425" data-source-node="n57"></a>Planned Delivery Date/Time Field</h1>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">This field represents the date/time that a shipment 
 is expected to arrive at customer. During carrier rating, the system will calculate 
 this date based on the Scheduled Ship Date and the carrier's days to deliver 
 requirements. </p>
