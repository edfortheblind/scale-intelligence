        <h4 data-source-node="n67">Summary...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n68">Yard Management refers to the tracking and movement of trailers within the “yard area” outside of the warehouse. The yard is used as a temporary storage area for truck trailers that are going to be received into your warehouse. When a truck arrives on your property, typically a guard (or other worker) would <i data-source-node="n69">check in</i> the trailer into your warehouse's yard area. They would perform this action using the full screen application. When you check in a trailer, you assign it to a specific yard location, whether it is an actual yard location or just an area in the yard where trailers are queued up until they are ready to be placed in their true destination yard location. Once a trailer is residing in a yard location, workers then can move the trailers to other locations in the yard, or move them to the Receiving Dock for processing. When inbound processing is completed for all a trailer's product, workers can move the trailer back to a yard location or have the guard check it out of the yard completely. To perform yard management processing, you need to associate trailer IDs to your receipts. You also have the option of scheduling appointments for arriving trailers.</p>

        <h4 data-source-node="n85">Terminology...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n86"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n87">Guard: The person who checks the trailer into the yard.  This person typically works in a guard shack at the entrance to the facility.  It is this person’s job to check the credentials of the trailers coming into the yard.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n88"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n89">Trailer: This represents the storage container that is attached to a truck entering your warehouse. It is this trailer that is placed into yard locations.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n90"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n91">Yard: The area outside of the warehouse where trailers are parked and stored.

</p>
        <p data-source-node="n92"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n93">Yard Check In: This represents the process by which a guard indicates in the system that a trailer has arrived and is granted access to the warehouse yard. The yard location/dock that the trailer is destined for can also assigned at this time.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n94"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n95">Yard Check Out: This is the process of having a trailer officially leave the yard, after processing for it is complete. Again, a guard typically performs the check out action.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n96"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n97">Yard Jockey: A worker who is in charge for moving trailers within the yard, and moving them to and from the dock doors.

</p>
        <p data-source-node="n98"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n99">Yard Location: This is a specific space in the yard where a trailer is placed upon entering the warehouse property. Typically, these locations are used to house inbound trailers, whose final destination is the Receiving Dock. However, outbound trailers can also be placed in a yard location, when they are waiting to be driven off of the warehouse property. Note that in SCALE configurations, yard locations are defined as dock locations.</p>
