
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1090" data-source-node="n57"></a>Creating Work Groups</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">A work group is a global category that describes one action required 
 to move product through the warehouse. Common work groups could include 
 receiving, picking, packing and shipping. Work groups are tied to work 
 types. They allow you to group together work types that are subsets of 
 one work group. For example, the work types order 
 picking and    batch picking 
 could be subsets of the work group    picking.</p>
