
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1480" data-source-node="n57"></a>Additional Criteria Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">Used to provide additional criteria when setting up a rate base. This additional criteria could be a specific company or type of shipment that the rate base is used for.   The rate base is used in LTL internal rating to determine the rates for calculating shipping charges.</p>
