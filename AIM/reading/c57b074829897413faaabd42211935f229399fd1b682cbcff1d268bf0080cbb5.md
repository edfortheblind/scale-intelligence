
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor678" data-source-node="n57"></a>Validated License Field</h1>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">The exporter's validated export license number 
 or general export symbol. This is a required license issued by the U.S. 
 Government authorizing the export of specific commodities. This license 
 is for a specific transaction or time period in which the export can take 
 place.</p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">You can find this field in the Shipment and Shipment Line Screens, and the fields on the shipment line are defaulted from the shipment.   This value   also can be interfaced in a shipment download and upload. This information is passed when rating international shipments, and appears on the international documentation.</p>
