
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1929" data-source-node="n57"></a>Show Data - Freight Cost Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field determines if shipping costs will 
 be displayed on the Trading Partner Management website. The default value 
 for this field is Y (Yes). You may not want to show freight costs if the 
 amount levied by the carrier is different than the amount you charge customers. 
 You can define this value on the Web System Values Window.</p>
