
        
        <h1 style="text-align: left;font-size: 12pt" data-source-node="n57"><a name="kanchor1282" data-source-node="n58"></a>Work Team</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n59">A work team is a group of individuals who share common characteristics such as work zones, equipment, or job responsibilities. To reduce setup time, you can create work teams for groups of employees instead of setting up individual user profiles for each person. During work execution, the system assigns tasks to teams based on the values defined in the work team record. You can also edit or delete work team records as needed.</p>
