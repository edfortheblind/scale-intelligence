
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor127" data-source-node="n57"></a>Requested Quantity Field</h1>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n58">The item quantity that the customer ordered. 
 This quantity is transmitted from the ERP with the order. The requested 
 quantity may be more than the quantity on the shipment. If an order is 
 associated with multiple shipments, the item quantity at the shipment 
 level may be less than the requested quantity. This typically occurs when 
 only part of the item quantity can be fulfilled. By splitting the order 
 across multiple shipments, the available quantity can be sent. The remaining 
 quantity can be processed in a later shipment.</p>
