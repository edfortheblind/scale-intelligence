
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1690" data-source-node="n57"></a>Apply to All Calendars Checkbox</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This checkbox indicates if a no ship day will be applied to all shipping 
 calendars. This value is commonly used for days when your warehouse is 
 closed and cannot ship product with any carrier. If you select this checkbox 
 for a particular date, it is considered a *default value. It is automatically 
 applied to all shipping calendar records. </p>
