
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1876" data-source-node="n57"></a>Converted Quantity Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field works in tandem with the Conversion Quantity Field, in that 
 it indicates the quantity of an item that is to be picked, which the system 
 has converted into the appropriate unit of measure. For example, suppose your conversion quantity is defined (on the Item 
 Unit of Measure) so that 10 eaches = 1 case. Suppose also that you have 
 a work instruction that calls for 50 eaches to be picked. The conversion 
 quantity would equal 50 eaches, while the converted 
 quantity would equal 5 cases.</p>
