
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1453" data-source-node="n57"></a>Permanent Location Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the permanent location for a particular item/unit 
 of measure. A permanent location identifies the one warehouse location 
 where the system can allocate or replenish that item/unit of measure.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n60">NOTE: The system can only allocate/locate non-inventory 
 items if they are associated with a permanent location.</p>
