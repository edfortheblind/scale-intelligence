
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1820" data-source-node="n57"></a>Receipt Type Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This is a free format field on a receipt record. 
 It is commonly used to identify what type of supplier sent the receipt, 
 such as manufacturer, vendor, etc. The system does not validate this value.</p>
