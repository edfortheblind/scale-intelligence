
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1895" data-source-node="n57"></a>Include Confirmed Shipping Loads Toggle</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">You can use this toggle to indicate that you want the system to display confirmed shipment loads in an insight screen search. Note that "confirmed" in this instance is defined as shipments with a status greater than or equal to load confirm pending. When you confirm a shipping load, the system will attempt to confirm any open shipments. You cannot confirm a shipment if it has not been released from its wave.</p>
