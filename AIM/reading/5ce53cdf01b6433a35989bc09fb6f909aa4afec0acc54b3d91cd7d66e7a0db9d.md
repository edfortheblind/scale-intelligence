
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1542" data-source-node="n57"></a>Unit of Measure Field / UM Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">In the system, all product is processed based on its quantity's unit 
 of measure. </p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n61">In Work...</h5>
        <p data-source-node="n62">The system can track work based on the UM 
 units completed during an hour. On the Estimated Work Rates Window, you 
 can use the Hourly Rate Field to identify how many UM units should be 
 processed for work type/work zone combination. For example, if the Hourly 
 Rate Field contains the value 5 and the Unit of Measure Field contains 
 the value Case the ideal processing output for the work type/work zone 
 combination would be 5 cases per hour. </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n63"> </p>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n64"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n65">For 
 Storage Containers...
</h5>
        <p data-source-node="n66">This field identifies a storage container used to hold product in a 
 location. All UMs are defined on the Unit of Measure Window. They are 
 associated with items on the Item Unit of Measure Window. Later, they 
 are associated with locations on the Item Location Capacity Window.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n67"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n68"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n69">In Shipments...</h5>
        <p data-source-node="n70">This field identifies the unit of measure that your company uses to identify the weight, volume or length of items or containers. All UMs are defined on the Unit of Measure Window.</p>
