
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor65" data-source-node="n57"></a>Using the Manifest Insight Screen</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">You can use the Manifest Insight Screen to search and manage the manifested shipping containers in your warehouse. You can identify the status of the Manifests: Open, Closed, and Transmit Status. Each of these statuses allow you to drill through the hierarchy to reach the amount of detail required for your research. </p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">When the manifesting process is started, SCALE transmits container-specific information, such as its weight, carrier, and carrier service, to the rating engine (Progistics). Progistics then contacts the carrier's server, and manifests the container. Progistics also retrieves the container's tracking number and its rate, and then transmits this information to SCALE. When a container is packed and closed, the system may automatically manifest it (based on user's packing preference record). Containers can be manually manifested on the Shipping Container Insight screen, and shipments can be manually manifested on the Shipment Insight screen.</p>

        <h4 data-source-node="n228"><a name="Screen" data-source-node="n229"></a>Screen Actions...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n230">The following actions are accessible from the list pane of Manifest Insight screen:</p>
        <p data-source-node="n231"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n232"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n233">Close</h5>
        <p data-source-node="n234">This action will close an open manifest.  Closing a manifest also provides options to print a manifest, print a summary barcode label, and transmit a manifest.  These options will be enabled depending on the carrier.  If there are ineligible containers on the manifest, you will  be able to choose among these options: Move the containers to another manifest for today, Move the containers to the next shipping day’s manifest, or Do not move containers from manifest.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n235"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n236"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n237">Print Container Manifest</h5>
        <p data-source-node="n238">This action allows to print the container manifest after the manifest has been closed. This action is not allowed with FedEx. For additional information on this, refer to <a href="bcb0a6ebc704fc613fcf9f8a284006af4bf15ccbd0ebd7f80fccf3c7717044a1.html" data-source-node="n239">Processing a Manifest</a> topic. </p>
        <p data-source-node="n240"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n241"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n242">View Containers</h5>
        <p data-source-node="n243">The action allows to view the shipping containers that are present in an open or closed manifest. Clicking on the action redirects to the Shipping Container Insight screen filtering on the Manifest name, Manifest status, Rating ID, and Shipper Code in the Manifest Criteria section of the filter pane. For additional information on this, refer to <a href="bcb0a6ebc704fc613fcf9f8a284006af4bf15ccbd0ebd7f80fccf3c7717044a1.html" data-source-node="n244">Processing a Manifest</a> topic. </p>
        <p data-source-node="n245"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n246"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n247">Print UPS Summary Barcode Label</h5>
        <p data-source-node="n248">This action allows to print the summary barcode label after the manifest has been closed.  This action is only available for UPS or UPS Worldease. The action remains enabled only if the manifest selected is closed and the carrier is UPS or UPS Worldease. For additional information on this, refer to <a href="bcb0a6ebc704fc613fcf9f8a284006af4bf15ccbd0ebd7f80fccf3c7717044a1.html" data-source-node="n249">Processing a Manifest</a> topic. </p>

(This is work around in case users are looking for <b data-source-node="n255">Transmit</b> action in the Manifest Insight screen).</p>
