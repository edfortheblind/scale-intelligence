
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor967" data-source-node="n57"></a>Manifest Status Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The Manifest status column in the List Pane Grid Fields of Manifest Insight Screen displays the state of the manifests as Open and Closed. The Open status indicates that the Manifest is not closed yet and the Closed status indicates that the Manifest is closed. See <a href="#" data-source-node="n59">Reviewing Manifest Status Values</a> for more information. </p>
