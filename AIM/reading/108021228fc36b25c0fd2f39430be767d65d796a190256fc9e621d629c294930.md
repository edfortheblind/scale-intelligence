
        
        <h1 style="text-align: left;font-size: 12pt" data-source-node="n57"><a name="kanchor500" data-source-node="n58"></a>Shift Time</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n59">Shift Time records are used to define and manage time tracking parameters specific to a warehouse. These settings help accurately record and monitor worker shifts within the designated warehouse environment.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <h5 data-source-node="n61">Create new Shift Time</h5>
        <ul data-source-node="n62">
            <li data-source-node="n63">
                <p data-source-node="n64"><b data-source-node="n65">Warehouse</b> - Choose the warehouse where the worker is assigned. This links the shift time data directly to the appropriate facility.</p>
            </li>
        </ul>
        <ul data-source-node="n66">
            <li data-source-node="n67">
                <p data-source-node="n68"><b data-source-node="n69">Shift</b>- Select the shift that corresponds to the worker’s scheduled working hours.</p>
            </li>
        </ul>
        <ul data-source-node="n70">
            <li data-source-node="n71">
                <p data-source-node="n72"><b data-source-node="n73">Description</b>- Provide a clear description of the shift to help identify its purpose or any special notes related to it.</p>
            </li>
        </ul>
        <ul data-source-node="n74">
            <li data-source-node="n75">
                <p data-source-node="n76"><b data-source-node="n77">Time Zone</b> - Specify the time zone relevant to the warehouse location.</p>
            </li>
        </ul>
        <ul data-source-node="n78">
            <li data-source-node="n79">
                <p data-source-node="n80"><b data-source-node="n81">Start and End Time</b> - Define the exact start and end times of the shift. This determines the duration of the worker’s scheduled shift within the warehouse.</p>
            </li>
        </ul>
