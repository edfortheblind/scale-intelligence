
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor955" data-source-node="n57"></a>Current Mappings Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the system attributes that the system will send 
 to Billing Management when the trigger is executed. These attributes will 
 be sent by grabbing the corresponding system attribute data and passing 
 it to Billing Management as a value for the corresponding mapped Billing 
 Management attribute.</p>
