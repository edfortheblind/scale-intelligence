
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1456" data-source-node="n57"></a>Reviewing Purchase Order Status Values</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">Purchase order status indicates the status of the purchase orders. There can be two status for purchase orders as mentioned below:</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <ul data-source-node="n60">
            <li data-source-node="n61">Open: When the purchase order is created, the status remains open. <br data-source-node="n62"><br data-source-node="n63"></li>
            <li data-source-node="n64">Closed: Once the purchase order lines are linked to a receipt, the status will be closed. Also the status gets set to closed after manually closing the purchase order.</li>
        </ul>
