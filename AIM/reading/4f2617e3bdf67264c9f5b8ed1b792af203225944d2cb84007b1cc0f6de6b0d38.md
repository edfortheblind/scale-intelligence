
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1483" data-source-node="n57"></a>Inventory Status Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the status of items contained in a location. The 
 system does not automatically look for a specific status when dealing 
 with inventory. The system will allocate and ship held inventory if you 
 do not set up your allocation rules to stop it. </p>
