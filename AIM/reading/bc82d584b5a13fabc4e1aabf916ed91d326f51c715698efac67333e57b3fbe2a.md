
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor386" data-source-node="n57"></a>Category Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the system function 
 where a warehouse alert originated. A warehouse alert identifies when 
 an error occurs in the system. For example, if the category is Background 
 Manifesting, the warehouse alert was generated during the scan and weigh 
 process.</p>
