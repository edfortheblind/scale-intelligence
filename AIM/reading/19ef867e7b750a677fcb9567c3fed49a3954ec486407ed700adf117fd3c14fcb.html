Processing Purchase Orders

Purchase orders indicate what item(s) your warehouse should be expecting to arrive at the receiving dock. PO's are used by a warehouse's suppliers/vendors to supply receipt information ahead of the arrival of the truck to their warehouse. You can create and manage purchase orders in SCALE on the Purchase Order Insight and Purchase Order Line Insight Screens. For example, suppose your warehouse's supplier is planning on sending you 500 pallets of Item A over the course of a year. They would first create a purchase order for Item A, adding a quantity of 500. When they are ready to ship some of Item A to your warehouse, they would create a shipment associated with this PO, which would indicate how many of the item they are going to send out. When the year is over, and all the Item A quantity has been shipped, the supplier would close the purchase order.

Data Archiving Notes...

  • Purchase orders will only be archived if there are no receipts tied to it. The receipt data archiving master will have to be run (to archive all receipts) for the purchase order archive master to work.

 

 

Searching For Purchase Order Records...

You can use the insight screen to research current purchase order activity.

  1. Access the Purchase Order Insight Screen.

  2. Enter the criteria you want to use to search for purchase order(s).

  3. Select the Include Closed POs toggle to have the system return purchase order records that are closed.

  4. Press the Apply Button. The system will display the records in the List Pane.

 

 

Closing Purchase Orders...

When a purchase order is closed, you can no longer add any more details to it. To do this, right-click a purchase order and select the Close action. The system will close the purchase order. The purchase order record will disappear from the insight screen grid (unless you had the "Include Closed POs" toggle selected when you performed your previous search query).

 

 

 

Canceling the Close of a Purchase Order...

If you cancel the closing of a purchase order, then you once again will be able to assign receipts to a purchase order. To do this, select a purchase order and choose the Cancel Close action. The system will ask you to confirm your action, and then re-open the purchase order.

 

 

 

Creating Receipts From Purchase Orders...

From the Receipt From Purchase Order Screen, you can generate a receipt from the information contained on a purchase order. See the topic Creating Receipts From Purchase Ordersfor more information.
