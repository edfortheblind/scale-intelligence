
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor556" data-source-node="n57"></a>User Type Field</h1>
        <h5 data-source-node="n58">In Trading Partner Management...</h5>
        <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n59">This field indicates the type of access a 
 web user will have on the Trading Partner Management website. There are 
 two types of web users: external and internal.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <table style="border-spacing: 1px 1px;margin-left: 0;margin-right: auto" cellspacing="0" border="1" data-source-node="n61">

            <tr data-source-node="n64">
                <td valign="top" class="td_11" data-source-node="n65">
                    <p data-source-node="n66"><i data-source-node="n67">External</i>
                    </p>
                </td>
                <td valign="top" class="td_11" data-source-node="n68">
                    <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n69">External web users include users of the TPM 
 website that are not employed by your organization. For example, a business 
 partner or supplier. External users can access the complete website.</p>
                    <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n70"> </p>
                </td>
            </tr>
            <tr data-source-node="n71">
                <td valign="top" class="td_11" data-source-node="n72">

                    </p>
                </td>
                <td valign="top" class="td_11" data-source-node="n75">
                    <p class="p_1" data-source-node="n76">Internal web users include employees of the 
 company that is hosting the TPM website. For example, employees using 
 the company intranet or working offsite. Internal users cannot access 
 the Email Notification Setup Page or the Statistics Page.</p>
                </td>
            </tr>
