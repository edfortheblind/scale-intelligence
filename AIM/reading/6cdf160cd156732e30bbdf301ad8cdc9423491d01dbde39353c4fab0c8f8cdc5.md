
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor978" data-source-node="n57"></a>Verification Method Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the method of work verification 
 at a location, such as a location name. A worker uses this 
 verification method to prove that work at a specific location has been 
 completed.</p>
