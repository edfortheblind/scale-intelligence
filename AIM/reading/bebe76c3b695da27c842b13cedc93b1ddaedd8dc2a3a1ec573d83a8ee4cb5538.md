
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor733" data-source-node="n57"></a>Movement Class Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the movement class associated 
 with an item or location. A movement class categorizes how frequently 
 an item is requested. Common examples of movement classes include "A" 
 movers, "B" movers, and "C" movers. You can use this 
 information to assign resources such as locations, equipment and work 
 teams based on movement class.</p>
