
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1215" data-source-node="n57"></a>Unloading Port Field</h1>
        <p class="p_1" style="font-weight: normal" data-source-node="n58">This field identifies the port where the ship 
 or plan docked when it was unloaded with export cargo. This value applies 
 to international shipments and appears on the Shipper's Export Declaration 
 Form.</p>
