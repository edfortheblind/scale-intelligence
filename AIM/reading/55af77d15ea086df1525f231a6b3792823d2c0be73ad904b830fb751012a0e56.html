Paperwork Process Summary

** Functionality **

Process Flows

 

 

 

Summary...

The paperwork process includes the generation and printing of paper documents and labels. You can obtain paperwork for all phases of the system's outbound and inbound processes, including such key processing points as running a wave, closing a container, or confirming a shipment/load. You can configure SCALE to use several different applications to render/print documents/labels, including SQL Server Reporting Services or Progistics. Keep in mind that if the shipped document templates do not meet your business needs, you can easily create custom documents by editing the template files. This allows you to meet a vendor's specific document requirements.

The following are key features of the paperwork functional area:

 

 

Custom Documents...

A number of predefined documents and labels are shipped with the application, such as pick lists, bills of lading, and container labels, just to name a few. Keep in mind that you can also create your own documents by copying a predefined template file and modifying it; or, you can create a completely new template. Document templates are stored in the Printing directory on your application server. Note that any custom templates that you create must also be stored in this directory.

 

 

 

Paperwork and Progistics...

Progistics generates certain parcel manifests and shipping container labels. Note that Progistics uses its own templates which cannot be modified.
