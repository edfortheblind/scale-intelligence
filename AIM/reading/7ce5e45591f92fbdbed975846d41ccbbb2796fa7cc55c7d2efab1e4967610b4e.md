
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor100" data-source-node="n57"></a>Changing Next Numbers</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The system generates the next number for all system-generated identifiers 
 (such as shipment number or parcel number). The system assigns the next 
 numbers in sequential order. Generally, next numbers are assigned by the 
 system during the wave process. The system increments these numbers by 
 one. All system-generated identifiers contain a default value that you 
 can change to reflect your business processes.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">WARNING! We strongly recommend that you do 
 not change a next number after it has been assigned by the system. The 
 system could assign a number multiple times, thus adversely affecting 
 system accuracy. See your system consultant before changing next numbers.</p>
