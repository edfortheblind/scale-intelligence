
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor587" data-source-node="n57"></a>Defining Pallet UM Conversion Criteria Records</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">Pallet UM conversion criteria records determine the parent containers that are eligible for the pallet UM conversion process. This process involves taking a full parent container (such as a pallet) and generating containers to represent its nested child containers, primarily for the purpose of printing vendor labels. You can initiate this conversion process during the Pallet UM Conversion wave step.</p>
