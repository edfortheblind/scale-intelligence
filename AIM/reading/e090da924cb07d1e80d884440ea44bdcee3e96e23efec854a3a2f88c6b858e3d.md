
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1385" data-source-node="n57"></a>Researching Item Inventory</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">You can use Trading Partner Management to research the inventory status 
 of items in a warehouse. This is helpful if you want to check if an item 
 is in stock before placing an order.</p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60"> </p>
        <h4 data-source-node="n61">Procedures...</h4>
        <p data-source-node="n62">Log into TPM. Choose the Inventory Status 
 option from the Trading Partner Management Main Menu. The system will 
 display the Inventory Status Inquiry Window.</p>
        <ol data-source-node="n63">
            <li value="1" data-source-node="n64">Enter the item 
 information, and press the Search Button. The system will display 
 the item(s) that matches your criteria. You can then see whether this 
 item is available in your warehouse's inventory. You can also click on 
 a specific item to view its detail information.</li>
        </ol>
