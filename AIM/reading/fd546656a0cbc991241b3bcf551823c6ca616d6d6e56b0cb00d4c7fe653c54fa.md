
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1972" data-source-node="n57"></a>Allocation Zone Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates the allocation zone associated with a location 
 or range of locations. This is helpful when you want to group your locations 
 into zones to be used during allocation.</p>
