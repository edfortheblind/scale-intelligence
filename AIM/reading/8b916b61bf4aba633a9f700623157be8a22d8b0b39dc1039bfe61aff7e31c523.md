
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor1067" data-source-node="n57"></a>Ship Quantity Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">The number of units associated with a container, shipment, shipment 
 line, or shipping load. The number of units ordered by the customer. On the Packing screen, this is the total amount of product that 
 needs to be shipped. As you pack against this amount, it never changes. 
 The Pack Quantity Field identifies how much product remains to be packed.</p>
