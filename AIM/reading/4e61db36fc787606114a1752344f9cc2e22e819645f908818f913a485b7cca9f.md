
        
        <h1 data-source-node="n56"><a name="kanchor627" data-source-node="n57"></a>Container Application Field</h1>
        <p data-source-node="n58">This field indicates to the system how many container(s) using a QC assignment record will be eligible for QC assignment. </p>
        <p data-source-node="n59"> </p>
        <p data-source-node="n60">For example:<br data-source-node="n61"><br data-source-node="n62">- <i data-source-node="n63">1 of 1 containers</i> indicates that the system will assign QC to all shipping containers that meet the assignment filter criteria.

<br data-source-node="n64"><br data-source-node="n65">- <i data-source-node="n66">1 of 10 containers</i> indicates that the system will assign QC to one out of every ten shipping containers that meet the assignment filter criteria.
<br data-source-node="n67"><br data-source-node="n68">Note that <i data-source-node="n69">1 of 0</i> and <i data-source-node="n70">NULL</i> are invalid values for this field.</p>
